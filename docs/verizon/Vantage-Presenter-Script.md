# Vantage: the presenter script

**Local: http://localhost:9110/vantage/** · hosted, once Simone gives the deploy go: `https://edge-platform-vantage.expedge.workers.dev/vantage/`

Fifteen beats in five acts, pressed as 22 presses: about twenty minutes with questions. Every number was measured on the running engine on 2026-09-30 (`docs/verizon/lanes/` L1, L2, L5); "about" marks a real spread between runs, and anything designed rather than built is called designed. **Vantage** is fictional; the phones and their prices are real; plans and promotions have real shapes under our names.

**The honesty ledger.** Real, say so: the arithmetic, rules, pins, windows, receipts, census figures, and the report as attribution. Labelled: the regional sales (synthetic, swapped for their warehouse; no account data), the rehearsed regions, and memory compressed for the room. Never: a lift or revenue number of ours, a bandit winner, "under 200 ms", "no limit", or another customer's name.

---

## Before you press anything

> "Two things before I start. Nothing on this page ranks anything: it sends what she does, paints what the engine answers, and every card carries its reason. And we curate what sits around the buy box, never the price: the buy box is off limits, and the page says so.
>
> One more, because a carrier will ask. We never vary price, discount, financing or credit by where someone lives. The only geography rule is availability: an offer for a service appears only where that service exists."

**Mechanics.** `Next →` runs the next press; `All beats` opens the list, the labelled assert buttons and the Compare tools; `Explain` (E) states the arithmetic before a beat and the receipts after it. Use it before beats 3 and 5.

## Act 1, beats 1 and 2: the arrival

Every carrier story in the market starts from a known subscriber. This one starts from a stranger.

### Beat 1. A stranger lands from a paid-social ad, in New York
**They see:** the hero and the four deals answer the ad: the launch hero, and the iPhone 18 Pro launch offer leading the four. The Arrival panel names New York, the grain, the census figure and the regional lean, labelled "region asserted, not observed".
**You say:**
> "She tapped an ad for the iPhone 18 Pro, and we know nothing about her. The first paint answers the ad and the place. The campaign lifts its own promotion, and the receipt itemises the lift. The rest leans on what people from New York actually buy: iPhone, premium. That lean is the carrier's own regional sales: synthetic here, labelled, and swapped for your warehouse. The census figure beside it is real and public. It explains the lean and never enters a score. Aggregate, never the individual; we curate, never price."

**Backed by:** launch offer first at 0.671, the Duo pre-order second (every run). New York, state grain, ACS 2024 1-year, $85,820 median household income, 425 synthetic sales; the campaign adds +0.066 on the launch offer's receipt; the record says the census is not scored. The launch hero scores 0.260 with the ad and 0.200 without: the campaign's +0.060.

### Beat 2. The same visitor, asserted in Texas
**They see:** the same four, reordered: the Galaxy S26 offer moves to second. The panel switches to Texas.
**You say:**
> "Same person, same ad. Only the region changed, and the page labels it asserted. Texas leans Android and core in the regional sales, so the Galaxy offer climbs. The ad still leads; without it, Texas opens on the Galaxy. A region is context. Her meters are still empty."

**Backed by:** launch 0.563, Galaxy S26 0.550; Texas, $79,721, 490 synthetic sales. Control: a Texas visitor with no ad opens on the Galaxy offer (0.550), with the launch offer third (0.497).

### 15c. Two clicks, and the day report
**They see:** two clicks, then Learned: the report's own window, then attribution per promotion and per module.
> "Each click is credited to the decision that held the container. Read what the report calls itself: attribution, not incrementality. Module engagement, attach, plan mix: the rows, with the decision behind each. Lift is yours to compute in your warehouse, against a holdout assigned by account so one account's lines never split."

Point at the window line: the report sums the folded hours of the UTC day. The fold runs every five minutes over hours closed at least five minutes, so the clicks just made appear once their hour has closed and the next fold has run, **never in the same second**. Measured: each click adds 1.00 to its promotion's clicks.

## What not to say

- **"Under 200 milliseconds."** It's 235 at the median for a stranger.
- **"No limit."** There are limits; this use case doesn't approach them.
- **Any lift, conversion or revenue number of ours.** The report is attribution, labelled.
- **That the engine runs a bandit or declares a winner.** It doesn't, and none is shown.
- **"You don't personalise today."** We're the moment before identity: a complement, never a critique. Let the page draw the contrast.
- **"More upgrades."** We show attach, plan and bundle mix, lines and module engagement.
- **"Games."** No hidden discount for the hesitant, no free-phone bait, no network-superiority copy, nothing implying subscriber data.
- **"Nothing depends on location."** Truer and narrower: no price, discount, financing or credit varies by where someone lives; the only geography rule is availability.
- **That the device signal is "never stored".** The fired seed shows on the receipt.
- **Any other customer's name.**
- **That the four reorder when she stops touching it.** The claim withdraws; the order usually holds.
- **That protection jumps above plans on upgrade**, or that a trade-in credit caps the S26's storage step: that phone carries no trade-in credit in this catalog.

## If something misbehaves

**Reset and reload, then carry on.** Beat 1 resets the tenant and starts a new visitor; any press can come first.

**A pale-yellow notice: "The page is keeping what it last showed".** The decision service didn't answer, and the page refuses to invent an order. Say exactly that: the page never makes up a result. Press the beat again.

**Beat 5 shows "Asking the engine…".** A slow read after the minute. Keep talking; it lands.

**Why these says receipts "are still being written".** The ledger records a few seconds after the answer; the beat-4 sentence waits for them.
