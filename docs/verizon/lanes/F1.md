## The report request (item 3)

**The engine finding.** The engine builds a day report for one whole UTC day only. `POST /v1/:tenant/learn/report` takes `{ date, brand?, policies? }` and has no slot or hour filter.

- **No hour of the day folded:** it reads the day's raw ledger and refuses past `REPORT_LIMITS.rawBytes`, 16 MiB (`src/learn/report.ts:27-30`, `hourly.ts:1894-1905`).
- **Any hour folded:** it sums the folded hours only (`publishAggregateDay`, `hourly.ts:1878-1888`). The hour in progress, and any closed hour not yet folded, are not in it.

So no request shape keeps a busy day inside the budget. Shaping can only make the refusal honest and put the window on screen.

**Request shape now.**

- Page to route: `POST /vantage/api/report {date: <UTC day of the clicks>, slot: "deals", build: true}` with `X-Tenant`.
- Route to engine, with the 60 s minted operator token: `POST /v1/vantage/learn/report {date}` (no brand, no policies), then `GET …/learn/report?date=<day>&slot=deals`, paged at 50 items.
- Then, only if that answered: `{date, slot}` for `pdp-modules`, `plan-pairing` and `hero`. These are reads of the built day and never rebuild it.

**Deviation, for the lead to rule.** I applied "the deals slot only" to the build request, the only request the budget can refuse. I kept the three paged reads that follow it, for these reasons:

- The slot does not change what the build reads. It is the same 16.8 MB, the whole UTC day.
- Each read is 10.7 to 14.9 KB and never touches the build budget.
- Dropping them would remove module engagement and plan mix. Design §9 beat 15, the presenter script 15c and L5's DOM check "and per module" all claim those.

## The engine's journey-stage vocabulary (`src/services/JourneyStage.ts`)

- **What the engine reports:** `exploring → thinking → deciding` (`JOURNEY_STAGES`, `:124`). These are stored as `early | mid | late` through `PERSISTED_STAGE` (`:133-138`).
- **What a published `journey` block may name:** only `thinking` and `deciding`. `exploring` is the floor (`:216-235`).
- **A second map, `STAGE_WORDS` (`:33-37`):** `early → exploring`, `mid → considering`, `late → deciding`. Content `journeyStageFit` is matched against this map (`decide.ts:213`), which is why the continue hero's fit is `['considering', 'deciding']`.
- **Vantage's thresholds** (`seed-vantage.mjs:139-141`): `thinking` at six product views in a visit; `deciding` at the first cart add or purchase. So "considering" is the receipt and fit word for the stage the engine reports as "thinking".
