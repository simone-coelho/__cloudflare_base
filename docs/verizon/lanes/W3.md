## 1. What the page does

A side-navigation page (the Tapestry page format: sticky numbered contents, a static block on narrow screens), light like the demo's site with the demo's dark "engine" panel for every instrument. Each instrument carries a "recomputed in your browser" tag; the top bar says "Recomputed in your browser. The demo's decisions come from the service."

1. **The one idea**, headed "The unit of state is the dimension value, not the click". Three sentences and one inline SVG: her vector, one candidate's tags in the same dimensions, the slot's weights, the dot product. A wide and a tall layout, chosen by width; the printed terms sum to the printed total, at four decimals. A short "why this unit" aside in the brief's own framing ("systems that keep state per event or per item usually need something learned per item…").
2. **Interest is a quantity.** Sliders for K, τ (5 s to 10 min), θ_in, θ_out (kept under θ_in, as the validator requires) and the action weight (with the published table as presets). Two live charts: a against touches (with the touch that enters marked) and a against time (a running clock at ×1, ×5 or ×20, a press button, the audience band, the dashed projection and t* marked). Readouts for R, R_eff, a, membership and the closed-form exit, with the formula written out in numbers. The three worked numbers at the current constants: 0.357, 0.625, 42.7 s.
3. **One click, four coordinates.** The Galaxy S26 event exactly as the demo sends it and the touches the engine extracts (with the band rule spelled out and `os` shown as ignored). A live shopper: five device buttons, meters for every coordinate with entry and exit ticks, and the whole state as (R, t) per value. Sections 4 and 5 read the same shopper.
4. **Scoring 10,000 candidates.** Two slots: deals (17 promotions, take 4) and the phones rail (16 phones, `POST /sort`, take 6, her own vector only). Editable per-slot weights, the brand cap, a pin, the asserted intent. Every row writes its sum out term by term, counts its lookups and multiply-adds, and names its outcome (container, yielded, failed eligibility, slot emptied). The standing text and the pin control both say, before anyone clicks, that an ineligible pinned piece empties the whole slot, as the engine's current behaviour; pinning a gated piece shows it, with the reason. "Run it on 10,000 synthetic candidates" counts the multiply-adds and the sort's comparisons; it does not time anything.
5. **Cold start as a prior.** New York and Texas synthetic sales, unit by unit, as shares (labelled synthetic → warehouse). The λ curve against Σa with a kBlend slider (the compiled default of 1 drawn faint). Her blended vector value by value. The two arrival seeds for the chosen arrival and device. A stranger's receipt for the launch offer at λ = 1, stating that its driver values are the region's shares, not her affinity, and checked against the measured receipt. The hinge as a chart across her presses, with the page-derived nature of the demo's "superseded" sentence stated. An optional button reads this request's region from the demo's credential-free cold-start read.
6. **Two memories, two speeds.** Brand (τ slider, 60 s for the room) beside ecosystem (14 days, 90 days per the design, or an hour), with live traces, closed-form exits and the sentence "Samsung claim withdrawn; Android holds". Labelled "compressed for the room; production runs days".
7. **The chain, end to end** (the worked example, unnumbered in the contents, between 6 and 7). Beats 1 to 5 recomputed step by step: events and touches, her vector, λ and the blend, the four and the ranking with every sum written out. Beside each step, the measured row from the facts sheet, shown only under the conditions it was measured in (New York, from the ad, no platform header). The timing sliders' defaults are labelled as fitted defaults, not as the suite's pace (§3).
8. **Learning what works.** Sliders for n, s, p0, n0, γ and the base; p̂, the raw and clamped lift, lift^γ and the score; at γ = 0 the sentence "Records from day one; moves nothing until you turn this". A p̂-against-n chart and the six-level ladder with illustrative counts the reader sets.
9. **The knobs are the model.** The three documents as served (registry r3, slots r51, learn r3), every knob annotated in one sentence. The section opens on the ledger's model line.
10. **The receipt.** The real receipt from the facts sheet, every field annotated; the recompute from the receipt and from the documents; a table showing all ten of the receipt's candidates reproduced by the page. A link to the demo's Explain panel.
11. **What your catalog needs.** One sentence on per product (attributes) against per slot (weights, rules); piece fields; exactly what happens to an unknown value (not scored, not flagged) and to an unknown product id (named in the answer); the dimension-to-field mapping and a promotion feed's fields and what the engine does with each (facts sheet §11, under a neutral title that names no carrier).
12. **The honesty ledger.** Real, labelled, and what this page is. Beside the way back to the demo, one line names the two companion notes by title only: *Why the unit of state is the dimension value, not the click* and *How the Vantage signal system works*.

**Tone.** No sentence describes, characterises or compares the customer's own system; the "why" is framed as our reasoning. No product adjectives. The model line reads "No model is called at decision time; every term on the receipt is arithmetic over the published documents", in the ledger and at the head of section 8 (the coordinator's wording, 2026-10-01, so the page and the two notes agree); the intro chip carries its first clause. Nothing more is said about models. The visible page cites code paths only; internal document paths and lane labels stay in this note and in code comments.

**Fitted timings.** The worked example defaults to touches 3.5 s apart, the hinge read 2.6 s after the third touch, and the re-ask 52 s after the third touch. These are fitted defaults, and the page labels them so. They are not the suite's pace: FACTS §1 has the suite's touches landing 1 to 2.5 s apart, read about a second later. Fitted, they reproduce the suite's third touch, hinge and exit exactly. Two residuals come from read latency, not arithmetic: touches 1 and 2 read 0.352 and 0.513 there against 0.357 and 0.519 here, and the Pixel offer 0.302 there against 0.303 here, because Google decays the same way before the hinge read. The facts sheet's "re-asked at 52 s" is read as 52 s after the third Samsung touch; that gives 0.398 exactly, where 52 s after the hinge would give 0.388, and the coordinator confirmed it on 2026-10-01. The gap slider states the arithmetic crossing window computed from the published constants: evenly spaced, three touches cross 0.60 only if the first and third land within 13.1 s. That holds only for even spacing read at the third touch; uneven spacing gives 9.75 to 21.4 s, and the suite's one-second read lag gives 11.0 s. The 2.6 s label quotes what the page prints at that setting, 0.303 for the Pixel offer, against the hosted 0.302.

No figure on the page comes from the seed script's comments; R2's two (a calibration remark on the lines weight and a presenter-pace range) were cut.

## 4. Posture

- No credential and no storage. No HTML is built from strings: every node is created and filled with `textContent`.
- A CSP meta tag: scripts from self only; styles from self and Google Fonts; no inline script or style.
- The one optional network call is `GET /vantage/api/coldstart` with `X-Tenant: vantage` only, `credentials: 'omit'`, same origin. Its answer is validated field by field (region key pattern, known dimensions and values, finite shares in [0, 1]) before use; anything else is dropped and the seeded numbers stay.
- The page loads Inter and JetBrains Mono from Google Fonts, as the demo's pages do.
