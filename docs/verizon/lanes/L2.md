# Lane L2: the two pages

**Status, 2026-09-30:** built, and both pages render from the live snapshot on lane L1's worker (`localhost:9110`, `REFLEX_HOST=session`, seeded, with lane L4's seeds and regional lean in force). All 22 presses of the walkthrough were run headless against that worker at 1920×1080, 1440×800 and 1366×768: 90 computed-visibility asserts per size, no failure, no console error. Nothing is committed, pushed or deployed. The page holds no credential.

## 2. Run it

- **Live:** open `http://localhost:9110/vantage/` (the worker serves `public/`). Press **Next**, or a beat from **All beats**. `?debug=1` exposes `window.__vantage` (`go(i)`, `beats()`, `state`) for the rehearsal suite. `?wait=N` shortens beat 5's minute for rehearsal only.
- **Phone page:** `http://localhost:9110/vantage/phone?id=galaxy-s26`. A direct load is a real arrival: it mints a visitor, sends her view and renders the live answer.
- **Fixture mode:** `?fixtures=1` replays `fixtures/engine.json`, labelled on screen. It is for layout only.
- **Headless walk (how the screenshots were made):** a CDP driver presses every beat through `window.__vantage.go(i)` and asserts computed visibility (style chain and bounding box, never attributes), no horizontal overflow, no broken image, the off-limits label, and that the page can scroll clear of the director. It resets the tenant with `POST /vantage/api/reset` at the end. The scripts are in the session scratchpad, not the repo.

## 3. Identity, transport and the event contract

Transport is the Top Offers pattern. Every call carries `X-Tenant: vantage`; shopper calls carry `X-SDK-Key: demo-site` and `X-Shopper-Session`. The session is minted first (`POST /v1/vantage/identity/session`), then her explicit consent (`POST /realtime/session/preferences`, tracking and personalisation on). Actions go to `POST /realtime/action` as `{ userId, sessionId, source: "vantage-demo", type, data, entry? }`; the landing `entry` (`utmSource`, `utmMedium`, `utmTerm`) rides on every action and every snapshot of the visit. Every snapshot asserts `ctx.intent` (default `new`) and, when asserted, `ctx.region`. The page sends no `pageInstance` and no `rendered`, so measurement stays `served-v1`.

The shapes below are lane L1's beat documents, as measured. Custom events name their action in `event`.

Beats publish by name through `POST /vantage/api/beat` and `/reset`.

## 7. Recorded decisions and what the design must change

1. **Phone URL.** The page lives at `/vantage/phone?id=<id>`. The path form `/vantage/phone/<id>` answers 404 on the worker today; one line in lane L1's route serving `phone.html` for `/vantage/phone/*` restores it, and the page already boots from either.
2. **Presses.** Fifteen beats are 22 presses so that each press changes one surface: 9a/9b, 10a to 10d (her answer on the phone page, then on the homepage, twice), 14a/14b, 15a to 15c.
3. **Back is not a new view.** Returning to a page (the browser's Back, drawn as a chip while the hand presses it, or a `back-phone` link) restores it and asks again without an event. Otherwise each return to the phone page is a product view, the sixth lands at 10a, and the continue hero takes the hero from the switch hero at 10b. Opening a phone from a card is always a view.
4. **Act 4 is seen as a new line.** Beat 11 sets her answer back to "new line", on screen and in the session log, as lane L1 measured beats 11 to 15. An upgrader keeps the Black Friday switcher offer (gated to new) out of the four.
5. **Beat 5 promises no reorder.** The minute is on screen, the meters are read before and after, and the sentence names the claim that fell under 0.45 and the lean that held. The four may keep or change their order (both were measured); the rail is the visual when the same answer re-sorts it. The `/realtime/ws` push stays behind `?exit=1`.
6. **Regions.** Acts 1 to 3 run in New York; Texas is beat 2's contrast; beats 3 and 15a return to New York on screen; 5G Home is shown in New York and withdrawn in Montana. The cold start is read with `?region=`; `?metro=` passes through. There is no ZIP override.
7. **Measurement.** No `pageInstance` and no `rendered`: measurement stays `served-v1`, as Top Offers. Clicks inside nested decided containers credit each level (a plan card and its module).
8. **Content the page supplies, labelled as fixture:** phone ratings, specs and colour shades (`phones.json` names colours only; the Duo, S26+ and Flip8 have no specs and show "—"), perk bodies and accessory items. Trade-in per plan is read from the cited offer's own plan line and `phones.json`; `plans.json` has no per-plan trade-in values.
9. **Copy.** The static blocks quote `plans.json` at load (5G Home $35, Mobile + Home $75, Rewards $40). Notes in content files are shown without their source citations.
10. **Images.** Phones render as `<picture>`: `.webp`, falling back to `.png`, never the white-ground `.jpg`. Promotions resolve by catalog id through the manifest; heroes sit on the page's gradient; a piece marked `imagePending` gets the page's labelled placeholder; a broken image is replaced, never left.
