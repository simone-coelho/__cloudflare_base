### The request-level suite

1. **The page loads.** The shells as a browser navigates. Node's `fetch` drops `Sec-Fetch-*`, so these go through `node:http`. The path form is reported as information. Then the 18 beat documents and the data files.
2. **No credential.** No token file is served. No served document, and none of the 61 files this demo ships, carries a JWT, bearer literal or capability. The page source makes no `/content`, `/config` or `/ops` call and has no `Authorization` header. It has exactly three fetch sites; `api()` and `demoRoute()` send `X-Tenant: TENANT`, and `TENANT` is `'vantage'`.
3. **The unsigned-read trap and the closed doors.**
   - `/content/catalog` and `/content/slots` refuse an unsigned read (401).
   - The beat route refuses an unknown name and a mismatched kind (400).
   - The prior seed door refuses a caller with no token (401).
   - The receipt refuses a caller with no session (401).
4. **The beats, exactly as the page sends them.**
   - Session, consent, every event with the landing `entry`, snapshots with `entry`, `ctx.intent` and `ctx.region`, `/sort` and `/realtime/reflex`.
   - **The page's `creditDecided()` clicks:** one `content_click` per decided container around a pressed control. They move `contentType`, and leaving them out changes the demo (finding 2).
   - Beats by name, and receipts carrying asserted context with its provenance. The hero's campaign seed (F1).
   - The cold start for New York, Texas and Montana. The region pair, with a no-ad control in Texas. The covered and uncovered pair, on the phone page and the homepage.
   - The day report on either of its paths, and a final reset checked by a fresh arrival.
5. **Refusals are visible.** A refused event is retried once and named at the end, never absorbed.

### The DOM suite

After every press it asks, as the Top Offers DOM suite does:

- **Did the service answer?** The press landed, neither engine-down notice is visible, and no API call during the press failed.
- **Is it legible?** The before/now row is visible and filled; on the phones grid, her meters moved. The director's sentence is on screen and matches the beat. The surface it concerns is in the viewport. There is no overflow and no broken image.
- **Did the named outcome happen?** L2's walkthrough, in titles read off the screen.
  - Geometry: the meter's bar crossing its tick, and the deals module's box above the hero at 15b.
  - Text on screen: the hero's "campaign +0.060", "region asserted, not observed", the pinned receipt, the continue hero's $198.55, and the report's own window line.

It presses Compare where the script uses it: Fresh visitor at beat 6, Streaming shopper at beat 8. At the end it checks the wire: every API call carried `X-Tenant: vantage`, none went to `/content`, `/config` or `/ops`, none carried `Authorization`, and there was no script error.

Visibility is the computed style up the ancestor chain (`display`, `visibility`, `opacity`) plus a real layout box, never an attribute.

## 4. Findings, and what other lanes or the design must change

1. **L2, fixed by F1: Compare's streaming shopper left her phone page holding his state.** Her 9a before row was his page; it is now hers.
   - **Residual for F1:** the address bar still shows his page, `/vantage/phone?id=iphone-18-pro-max`, until her next navigation. Both 1920 runs printed it. A reload then boots his phone page as a direct visit.
2. **The request-level replay must send the page's `content_click`s.** Without them, protection measured above Pair with a plan at 10c (L1's table). With them, both suites see protection fourth under Pair with a plan, as L2's walk did. **Design §9 beat 10 and L1's table should drop the claim**; the lead ruled it after R1.
3. **F1, page defect: beat 5's sentence when the minute starts late.** `exitSentence()`'s "fading" branch says "still above the 0.45 exit threshold" when the value is already under it. The 23:07 run printed "brand samsung 0.42 → 0.25 … still above the 0.45 exit threshold". This is for F1 to fix after this run, by the lead's ruling.
4. **Engine: the 9110 ledger consumer stalled twice on the session host.** Each time every receipt answered 202 afterwards, and L1 restarted the worker after the first.
   - **At 22:11** (F1 finding 3). The consumer's last batches sit among this lane's 15c report calls and the reset that followed.
   - **At about 23:16.** The last `QUEUE events-vantage` line is 4726 in `wrangler-9110-final3.log`, during the clean DOM run's last presses.
   - Both followed a beat-15c report build, amid floods of the `LearnStats.priorsFor` "Configuration publication authority unavailable" errors (finding 5).
   - **Hypothesis, not established:** an isolate restart from those errors leaves a ledger owner forward unsettled. That would be L4's seat: `forwardLedgerOperation` waits without a timeout on the owner (`src/identity/sessionAuthority.ts`).
   - **Beside F1's owner-path finding 4:** receipt reads answered 503 once each at beats 12 and 14b (23:07 run), and once at 14a (23:13 run).
5. **Engine: every report build raises "Configuration publication authority unavailable"** in `LearnStats.priorsFor` (`src/durable-objects/LearnStats.ts:855`, through `readPinnedPublication`). Locally the runtime restarts, and an event landing in that second gets 503 "Your worker restarted mid-request": 1 in 2 clicks fired during a build. The page clicks before it builds, and the page suite waits 4 s after its own pre-click read.
6. **Engine: the day report.**
   - **The raw-day build is capped at `REPORT_LIMITS.rawBytes`, 16 MiB** (`src/learn/report.ts`), and past it the build answers 413. F1 measured the refusal at 16,802,288 bytes. **Correction:** this lane first named the 800-object cap; the budget that refused was `rawBytes`.
   - The build is one whole UTC day, with no slot or hour filter (F1). Once any hour is folded it sums the folded hours only (`runDayReport`, `src/learn/hourly.ts`). So a click appears once its hour has closed, the five-minute grace has passed and the next fold has run, never in the same second. The script and the resume say so.
   - **The local cron, sixteen runs by the lead's rulings,** with `curl "http://localhost:9110/cdn-cgi/handler/scheduled?cron=*/5+*+*+*+*"`. Each run's effects match the hosted cron: quarantined ledger records expired, one monitor decision per provisioned tenant (each failing locally with 10 to 12 problems), the warehouse slice, the fold, one day report republished.
     - The first run (22:03) folded two empty hours.
     - Then fifteen runs (22:06–22:09), the cap. Runs 1–10 folded empty hours. Run 11 folded 2,055 decisions from 2026-09-30. Run 12 folded one of today's closed hours (3,928 decisions), while the other **failed and was skipped**; runs 13–15 retried it, logging "hourly fold hours failed and skipped 1".
     - Since run 12 the report builds from folded hours in about 0.5 s. It names the unfolded hours in its window line.
   - **A busy hour can fail to fold every time, and the report then leaves it out**, listing it as missing. A heavy rehearsal hour on the hosted worker could drop out of beat 15 the same way. To find the cause, log `failed[].error` where `src/index.ts`'s scheduled handler calls `catchUp`.
7. **Engine, session host: an audience flag outlives its score.** At the re-ask brand samsung reads about 0.40, yet `brand_samsung_affinity` stays listed until her next event. Ranking reads the score; the script's Q&A says so.
8. **Design §5: "the campaign's 0.2 applied".** On screen the receipts show contributions: +0.066 on the launch offer and +0.060 on the hero. 0.2 is the seed's weight, times each slot's campaign weight of 0.3, and the launch offer's promotion boost.
9. **Design §3: the storage maths on the Galaxy S26.** The trade-in cap sentence never appears, because the S26 has no trade-in credit in `phones.json`. Beat 7 shows only "+$5.56/mo over 36 months", and the script claims no cap.
10. **L1, content: the Pixel 11 launch offer's headline says "on us with a new line"**, but its rule and terms also admit an upgrade with a trade-in. So it sits second at 10d, beside "the new-line offers are gone". The script now says "the offers that need a new line leave" and gives the answer. A headline naming both would remove the question.
11. **The phone path form.** A browser navigation to `/vantage/phone/<id>` gets the root page, and F1 retired the route. The page uses `?id=`.
12. **L2 §3 and design §3: "`/sort` answers only once the visitor has a captured event"** is no longer true: it answered 200 with the site's order before any event.
13. **Local latencies are not stage figures.** Under `wrangler dev` a stranger's first snapshot took about 0.8 to 1.0 s. The script uses the design's measured edge figures.
14. **For anyone writing a probe:** replay the page's events exactly, and read report counts as decayed counts (a click adds about 1.00 to `s`).
