## 2. The regional lean

The data brief found no public regional OS or brand data, so the lean is the carrier's own sales, here synthetic and labelled `synthetic → warehouse`. The rehearsed regions both have real ACS 2024 1-year state rows:

| Region | Synthetic sales | Leaders (share, leader = 1) | Census |
|---|---|---|---|
| **US-TX** (A) | 400 phones + 90 home-internet | samsung 1 · apple 0.938; android 1 · ios 0.6; core 1 · premium 0.744; home-internet 0.225 | Texas $79,721 / $313,200 |
| **US-NY** (B) | 400 phones + 25 home-internet | apple 1 · samsung 0.438; ios 1 · android 0.667; premium 1 · core 0.5; home-internet 0.063 | New York $85,820 / $449,800 |

**How it is written.** `POST /vantage/api/coldstart/prior` takes one region per call. It resets that region's `RegionTrend` object, ingests one frame per synthetic sale through the object's own door, publishes, records a seed marker in KV (`demo:vantage:region-seed:v1`) and rolls the tenant up into `US` and everyone.

- A rerun replaces the data; it never adds.
- Access: operator JWT with a grant on `vantage`, demo profile, `ENVIRONMENT=development`, US state keys only.
- The vocabulary is closed: category, brand, ecosystem and priceBand. No census, households, lines or intent are ever seeded.
- Each call makes one object call per synthetic sale (about 430–490 per region), within the paid-plan subrequest limit, so keep one region per call.
- The object decays with τ 24 h. Shares survive uniform decay, but the venue's live traffic gains weight as the seed ages, so **reseed the morning of a rehearsal**. No redeploy follows a reseed: nothing under `public/` changes.

## 3. The cold-start panel (for L2)

`GET /vantage/api/coldstart[?region=US-TX][&metro=35620]` with `X-Tenant: vantage`. It needs no credential. It answers 404 unless `DEPLOYMENT_PROFILE=demo` and the worker provisions `vantage`.

**Region.** The asserted `region` wins (the same rule as the snapshot's prior on the demo profile); otherwise the edge region is used. The postal code is never read: IP location on cellular networks is off by about 200 km at the median.

**Grain.**

- Metro is used only when asserted, and only if `geo_xref` places it in the region's state.
- Otherwise state; otherwise national, with `census.fellBack` naming why.
- Estimate labels: state and metro rows read "ACS 2024 1-year", a ZIP row would read "ACS 2020–2024 5-year", and proxy rows read "representative: a state-level proxy (…)". The Dallas metro row is such a proxy.

**The lean.**

- `lean.dataSource` is `synthetic` only when the region that answered was seeded, or when the answer is a rollup that includes a seeded region.
- A region that holds only live demo traffic reads `observed on this demo worker only`. This machine's edge region, US-NC, answered that way with 206 events of measurement traffic.

## 4. The demo-only region override

`service.ts`: on `DEPLOYMENT_PROFILE=demo` only, with tracking consent, the snapshot's `ctx.region` also selects the regional prior when it is a region key (`^[A-Z]{2}(-[A-Z0-9]{1,3})?$`). ### 5.2 The platform seed (general engine feature)

- `platformOf(headers)` reads `Sec-CH-UA-Platform` first, then the user agent, and returns `ios`, `android` or null. It never returns a model or version, and the value is never stored.
- The snapshot passes it under the tracking gate, like `entry`.
- A `platform` rule names `ios` or `android` exactly.
- `dampen` is a list of up to 4 `{dimension, value, factor 0..1}`. Each interest her **own** memberships hold multiplies the rule's weight. It is never read from the regional prior or from asserted context, because asserted context may gate but never re-rank. The receipt lists every applied dampen.
- The list form exists because the first measurement showed that a non-yielding OS seed at 0.5 kept an iPhone visitor on iOS offers for more than 6 Android presses. With a yield on the other ecosystem her own browsing wins at press 3.

### 5.4 Page obligations (L2)

- Send `X-Tenant: vantage` on every call, including `/vantage/api/coldstart`.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Peak | 0.376 | 0.307 | 0.172 | 0.172 |
| At the alarm (+1.5 s) | 0.328 | 0.262 | 0.172 | 0.172 |

The brand claim withdraws and android holds, but **the order of the four does not change**. Ranking is continuous in affinity, and Galaxy offers stay ahead on ecosystem, band and category until brand falls to about zero, where catalogue order keeps Galaxy first. The stand-in behaved the same: 0.609 at peak, 0.57 at the alarm, order held.

A reorder at the exit would need a competitor within `w_brand × (a_peak − 0.45)` ≈ 0.045 of the Galaxy offer at peak. **Verdict.** The alarm and the frame are real and on time on the object host. As scripted, "the module withdraws its claim" is true of the brand driver and the meters, not of the order. Under the lead's ruling (session host), beat 5 is a timed re-ask: both hosts decay lazily at read time, so a re-ask about 43 s after the last press shows `brand samsung` below 0.45 on the meter and on the Galaxy receipts, with android held. Say so, and do not promise a reorder unless L1 tunes one.

## 7. The object-host ledger stall (L1 finding 9), one-hour box

It was not reproduced on the L4 worker running source byte-identical to the working tree (`diff -rq` clean), with L1's `max_concurrency = 6`. Every load was consumed in full by the first check, 5 to 15 s after the last request:

- 40 quick snapshots.
- 15 rounds of view, snapshot and receipt read with a socket held open, then a burst of 30.
- 40 more in the same process.
- 25 rounds of view, snapshot and `POST /sort`.
- 30 parallel pairs from two shoppers.

Excluded:

- **Single-owner load.**
- **Lock-order deadlock between batches.** `ledgerOperationOwners` sorts owners by tenant, then browser before person, then subject (`sessionAuthority.ts:117`), so batches always take object chains in one order.
- **Consumer concurrency.**
- **The sort stream.**
- **ODP.** It is off for non-default tenants without a connector configuration.

What remains different is L1's environment (the repo `.dev.vars`, not read by this lane) and its long-lived persisted state. The seat still points where L1 said: `forwardLedgerOperation` awaits the owner's `/identity/ledger`, which runs inside that object's `serialize()` chain (`ShopperReflex.ts:471-497`). Any earlier chain holder that never settles blocks every later batch, with no timeout.

Next step when it stalls: note the last `QUEUE events-vantage N/N` log line, then test whether the same shopper's `/realtime/reflex` still answers. If it hangs, the object's chain is blocked. A fix cannot be sized until it is caught. The narrow candidate is a timeout on the owner forward, so the batch retries instead of hanging; it needs a ruling and was not built.

## 8. What the design must change (recorded, not applied)

1. **The page re-sends the landing entry** on every snapshot of the visit (§5.4).
2. **Deals `merchandising.promotion` 0.1** in the opening slots, or the ad does not change the Texas first paint (§5.3).
3. **The hinge is performed in region A (Texas).** New York is the contrast first paint; its hinge lands at press 3–4, and at 15 s per press only at 4.
4. **Beat 5** as §6: meter and receipt, no reorder; a timed re-ask on the session host.
5. **An Android phone in Texas opens on Galaxy, not the ad.** This is correct arithmetic. Say it if a phone is used on stage; a desktop sends no platform.
6. **Forward-only deploys.** A slots document carrying `platform` or `dampen` fails the whole-document stored parse on any older build (`kinds.ts:435`, fail closed, and the tenant then answers 503). Before any rollback, republish slots without them.
7. **Docs** outside this lane: `docs/kit/03-payload-schemas.md` "Contextual seed rules" should list `platform` and `dampen`.
