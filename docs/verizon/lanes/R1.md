Not ready to deploy yet, but close. I found no credential in a page or a commit, and the page never ranks. Nothing ties geography, census or OS to price, financing, credit or eligibility beyond the two named rules. The engine changes are general or gated as ruled, the unit checks are green, and the request-level suite passes. Four things must change first.

1. Correct the footer line and the "never stored" wording, or stop persisting the platform value. A presenter will say both.
2. Fix the before row after Compare and the report build that returns 413 on a busy day. Then make beat 10's protection claim hold, or soften it.
3. Add a test for the cold-start route and a route-level test of receipt ownership.
4. Get the DOM suite passing in a run of its own.

The manifest gap and N4–N10 can follow. Creating the worker, its queues and its secrets waits for Simone's go.
