## 0. Purpose and posture

Verizon is about to sign a partnership with us on another matter. This demo is not part of it and sells nothing. They want to see how we think about personalization and whether we are the kind of partner a lighthouse customer innovates with. So the posture is **show, comprehensively**: the most complete surface the engine can carry, every decision explained on screen, nothing to protect and nothing to hit.

What we are showing, in one sentence: **a stranger arrives from an ad and the page opens on what people like her, from where she is, actually buy; she picks a phone and the page reorganises itself around what she is telling it, moving her toward more storage, a richer plan, a second line, a home bundle, without ever touching the price.**

The rules every demo in this repository already obeys apply unchanged (`docs/demos/BOOTSTRAP.md` §0, §12): the page never ranks; every beat is a decision that came back from the service; every figure on stage is real or labelled; nothing changes without a press; no credential in the page; verify through the APIs, never the pages.

## 2. The story: five acts

**Act 1, the arrival.** A stranger taps a paid-social ad for a phone. The first paint answers the ad and the region: real region from the request, real census income band, and the regional device lean from the carrier's own sales (synthetic, labelled, swap-to-warehouse). The same visitor in another region opens differently; only the region changed. The cold-start panel names the grain used and says "aggregate, never the individual; we curate, never price."

**Act 2, the visitor earns it.** She browses a Galaxy, then a Pixel. The meters move on brand, OS and price band. The evidence gate holds the defaults, then releases. Then the hinge: the ad said iPhone, she is looking at Android, and the deals module follows her. The receipt reads "campaign context superseded by observed behaviour."

**Act 3, the phone page follows intent.** She opens the Galaxy. Below the buy box sit six modules in the template order. She compares storage and camera; "Storage & memory" rises, leading with 512 GB. She opens the hotspot perk; "Pair with a plan" rises, leading with the middle tier. She visits "add a line"; the family module rises with the four-line per-line price. Protection rises for a premium device. Two shoppers side by side.

**Act 4, the merchandiser's day.** The launch promotion's window closes and the next promotion takes the container. Black Friday's "switch and save" arrives with no history and reaches the module on its metadata. The launch hero is pinned for everyone during launch week; the receipt says ranking was skipped. The home-internet bundle is eligible only where 5G Home is available: asserted region, fails closed; the same shopper in an uncovered region does not see it.

**Act 5, order and metric.** Tomorrow's homepage template moves the deals module to position one and the four do not change. The report: clicks per promotion and per module, labelled attribution; lift is computed in their warehouse against the holdout.

**What the room has not seen** (landscape brief, 2026-09-30). Every vendor's carrier story starts from a known subscriber: Pega's next-best-action already runs Verizon's engine at about a billion transactions a month, and Adobe's fourteen telco blueprints, Salesforce's agents, Google's and AWS's references all assume sign-in. No public demo restructures the page for a first-time anonymous visitor and prints the reason. And verizon.com itself, browsed anonymously that day, did not move: the researcher chose 512 GB and four lines and returned to the homepage, and neither the homepage, the phone page nor the plan bundles changed order. That is the contrast the demo draws without saying a word against anyone: *we did what you did, and the page followed.*

## 4. The engine documents ⟨research fills the vocabularies⟩

Tenant `vantage`, scope of its own; nothing touches `shn`, `coach` or any customer stamp.

**Registry (reflex document).** Eight dimensions, each mapped onto one of the engine's existing shapes so Meridian's instrument carries over with only its labels changed (data brief §3), exact-value vocabularies, each fed by named behaviours. Storage folds into the price band (toggling 512 GB moves the band); plan tier is expressed through `perk` and the plan pieces' own tags.

| Shape · dimension | Values | Moved by | Cold-start prior |
|---|---|---|---|
| broad · `category` | phones, plans, home-internet, accessories, watches-tablets | navigation and category views, searches | a small home-internet lean where the seeded regional sales show the bundle selling |
| narrow · `brand` | apple, samsung, google, motorola | phone card and phone page views, compare, the trade-in device | seeded regional sales |
| hue · `ecosystem` | ios, android | phone, accessory and watch views; the trade-in device | **the visitor's own OS family** from the request (a general `platform` seed; OS family only, never a model, never a fingerprint; its fired value shows on the receipt), halved when intent is switch, because loyalty to an ecosystem drops when people change carrier |
| band · `priceBand` | entry (under $400), core ($400–899), premium ($900 and up), on full retail | phone views, the storage step, price sort, certified pre-owned | seeded regional sales; the census figure explains the lean on the panel and never scores |
| need · `intent` (multi-valued) | new, switch, upgrade, add-line, byod, home | the toggle (asserted, labelled), switch / bring-your-phone / add-a-line pages, IMEI and address checks, the UTM campaign | the campaign seed from `utm_term` |
| need · `perk` (multi-valued) | streaming, music-games, hotspot-travel, international, family | perk views, the plan builder's starters | none |
| durable · `lines` | solo, duo, family | line selector, add a line, kids' watch, two devices in the cart | **none, by rule**: never from census household or family tables (doc 13 §10) |
| content · `contentType` | deal, compare, explainer, tool | deal tiles, compare, FAQs, the trade-in and coverage tools | none |

Journey stage is the engine's own stage, reported as exploring, thinking and deciding (`src/services/JourneyStage.ts:124`) and matched by content fits as exploring, considering and deciding (`STAGE_WORDS`, `:33-37`); Vantage sets thinking at six phone views and deciding at the first cart add. It is not a meter. Production decays would run about 14 days on intent, 60 on band and 90 on ecosystem and lines, since phones are financed over 36 months. **For the room, memory is compressed and labelled as such**: `brand` and `contentType` decay on a 60-second tau (so the exit beat happens while the presenter is talking), `priceBand`, `intent` and `perk` on about ten minutes, `ecosystem`, `lines` and `category` on the shipped 14 days. The two speeds are the story: the brand of the moment fades; the ecosystem lean holds.

Context cells, not dimensions: `channel` (paid-social, search, email, direct), `visit` (first, returning), `region` (from the request, at **state or metro grain**: IP location on cellular networks is off by about 200 km at the median, so the ZIP rung is never used for a mobile network). Action weights: the engine ships view 1, wishlist 2, add to cart 3, purchase 5 and the content events; Vantage's published registry adds page view 1, search 1.5, select, compare and filter 2, start order 3 (corrected 2026-10-01 from the facts sheet; the registry publishes them, the engine does not ship them).

**Catalog document.** Four kinds of piece, all with `type`, `tags`, `slotTypes`, `window`, `renderUrl`, and `eligibleWhen` where a rule applies:

- Promotions for `deals`: id, title, badge (`offerType`: trade-in, new-line, switch, flash, bundle, loyalty), image, tags across `brand`/`ecosystem`/`priceBand`/`intent`/`lines`/`category`, the promotion grammar as content (who, plan requirement, consideration, value over 36 months, what ends the credit, not combinable), windows relative to reset (launch offers about six to eight weeks, switch offers months, flash cuts days), `merchandising.promotion` on the launch promo, `eligibleWhen: { context: { intent: [...] } }` on intent-gated offers and `eligibleWhen: { regions: [...] }` on the home bundle, four defaults for the default arm. The launch promotion is the iPhone 18 Pro trade-in offer on a new line; the Black Friday arrival is the switcher offer with no trade-in (the 2025 precedent).
- Heroes for `hero`: `hero-launch` (default), `hero-switch` (tagged `intent: switch, byod`), `hero-family` (tagged `lines: family`), `hero-continue` (`journeyStageFit: [considering, deciding]`; its body is rendered by the page from the shopper's own selections, labelled "your selections, nothing inferred").
- Modules for `pdp-modules`: `storage-memory`, `plan-pairing`, `trade-in`, `protection`, `accessories`, `connected-devices`, `home-bundle` (eligible where covered), each tagged with the dimensions it serves and the journey stage it suits.
- Plans for `plan-pairing`: Simple and the three Unlimited tiers, four pieces tagged by `perk`, `lines` and `intent` (Simple carries switch).
- Phones for `phones-rail`: attributes travel on the sort request and on events (no catalog copy is required for `/sort`), drawn from `public/vantage/phones.json`.

**Slots document.** `hero` take 1 with a `stage` rule so the continue hero is eligible only once the shopper is considering (six phone views in a visit) or deciding (the first add to cart); `deals` take 4, `diversity` cap of two per `brand`, the promotion weight, and the W16 **`seeds`** block that lifts the campaign's promotion when the landing URL carries its `utm_term` (the arrival, §5); `pdp-modules` take 6, ordered, the template order as the baseline; `plan-pairing` take 3, ordered; `phones-rail` via `/sort` with the dials on the request. **No evidence gate.** QVC's five-page rule was QVC's; this demo's point is that a stranger is served from the first paint on population-level priors and the page then follows her. (Top Offers' page-side gate also records exposures nobody saw, `top-offers.js:307-318`; do not copy it.)

**Learn document.** `regional: { enabled: true, kBlend: 0.5, minEvents: 30 }`, the blend weight λ = K / (K + Σa) so the prior gives way as her own evidence grows (at kBlend 1 the Texas prior kept Samsung offers in the four after her brand claim had decayed, and moved three Act 3 claims). **Holdout 0 on stage**: assignment is hash-sticky and a 5 percent arm could put the presenter's own visitor in the default arm; the holdout is shown as a setting in beat 15, not run. γ = 0 (learning records, nothing moves until the dial does); exploration off; **`regional.enabled` set**, because a missing block means the prior is off (`service.ts:430`); the 21-day horizon as shipped.

**The receipt action.** The snapshot answer carries only a thin receipt (drivers, note, diversity); the seeds, merchandising, stage, freshness, fatigue and the regional blend live on the ledger record, readable today only through operator routes (`decide.ts:491-504`, `decisions.ts:389`). The vantage route therefore exposes a named, credential-free **receipt action** that returns one decision's record for the page's "why these" panel. Without it the hinge and the cold start cannot show their reasons.

**Beats as versioned publishes** through `src/routes/vantage.ts` (a fork of `topOffers.ts`): a closed list of beat names, each applying a document under `public/vantage/beats/`, a 60-second operator token minted server-side, the ordinary pipeline. No document is accepted from the page.

## 5. The cold start and the arrival, on Generation 2

Ruled from the code map (2026-09-30): the Generation 1 census cohort (`src/services/geo/cohort.ts`) reads Coach transactions and is not reachable from the content service; the Generation 2 **regional prior** (`src/reflex/regionTrend.ts`) blends this tenant's own regional touches through region, country and global, is off unless published, and reads only the edge region. So:

**As built (L4, 2026-09-30).** `scripts/seed-vantage-region.mjs` writes the synthetic sales through `POST /vantage/api/coldstart/prior` (operator grant, demo profile, development only; a rerun replaces): US-TX 490 sales leaning Samsung, Android and core with a home-internet lean of 0.225; US-NY 425 sales leaning Apple, iOS and premium. The prior's horizon is 24 hours, so it is reseeded on the morning of a rehearsal. `GET /vantage/api/coldstart` is the credential-free read for the panel: region and its source (asserted wins, then the edge; ZIP never read), the grain used (asserted metro checked against `geo_xref`, else state, else national with the reason named), the census figures with source and an estimate label, the lean with its `dataSource`, `scored.census: false`, and the sentence. The `ctx.region` override lives in `service.ts`, demo profile and tracking consent only; the record's `explain.regional` carries `asserted: true` and the cell keeps the edge region; five tests and a negative control. Measured on the opening documents: without the ad, Texas opens Galaxy S26 0.55, Fold8, then the launch offer 0.497; New York opens the launch offer 0.605, then the iPhone Duo. With the ad, Texas opens on the launch offer at 0.563.

- **The regional lean is the carrier's own sales, seeded.** A one-time seed writes synthetic regional device touches (brand, OS, price band) per rehearsed region into the `vantage` regional prior, labelled `dataSource: synthetic → warehouse`. Every US state and DC is seeded (2026-10-01), so the visitor's real region always has a lean: New York and Texas as measured (iPhone premium; Android core), the other 49 from a small documented model that tilts national shares by the state's census median income with moderate tilts, labelled synthetic like the rest. The suites rehearse on New York through the explicit override; beat 2 asserts Texas as the contrast (New York if the visitor is really in Texas). This is the honest and the stronger framing: "what your customers, from here, actually bought", exactly the Coach cohort's posture.
- **The visitor's own OS family** is the strongest per-visitor prior and it is free: the `platform` seed reads the OS family from the user agent or `Sec-CH-UA-Platform` at the snapshot and seeds `ecosystem` (a general engine feature, tested, never a model or a fingerprint; halved when intent is switch). **In reserve**, not built now: the network's ASN seeding `switch` when the visitor arrives on a competitor's mobile network, with copy that never names the network and never resolves an address to a subscriber.
- **Census is shown, not scored.** Real ACS income and home value from the shared D1 tables `geo_census` and `geo_xref` (`migrations/seed/seed_011_geo.sql`, NC plus 15 states plus national) appear on the cold-start panel with their vintage as the public explanation of the lean (state and metro rows are ACS 2024 1-year; any ZIP row is ACS 2020–2024 5-year, and the panel says so). No census term enters the score, so nothing ever ties a neighbourhood's income to a price, a plan or a financing decision.
- **The real region always leads** (ruled 2026-10-01 after Simone opened the hosted page from North Carolina and read "New York"). Beat 1 asserts nothing: the edge's region is what the panel names, "from the request", with its grain and census, and the hero and deals open on that region's prior. The identification is real even where the data behind it is synthetic, exactly the Coach cold start's honesty model, and a first paint never says a place the visitor is not. **A demo-only region override** exists for the Texas contrast beat and for explicit rehearsal flags (`?region=US-NY`), labelled "asserted, not observed", never the default: on `DEPLOYMENT_PROFILE=demo` only, an asserted `ctx.region` selects the regional prior; the engine's rule that an asserted region moves no affinity (`service.ts:485-490`) stands on every customer profile; refused off the demo profile, tested, named on the receipt as asserted.
**Arrival, as built.** `public/vantage/seeds.json`: `campaign_term iphone-18-pro → campaign` at weight 0.2; `platform ios / android → ecosystem` at weight 1 with a `dampen` list (0.5 when `intent` is switch; 0 against the other ecosystem), because a single-factor version kept iPhone visitors on iOS for more than six presses. The `deals` slot's promotion weight is 0.1 (at 0.25 the launch offer led Texas even without the ad). Measured: the first paint answers the ad in both regions on desktop and iPhone; Android takes the top at press 1 in Texas and press 3–4 in New York; an Android phone in Texas opens on Galaxy (own device plus region outweigh the 0.2 seed), which is correct. **So the hinge is rehearsed in New York, and Texas is beat 2's contrast.** The page sends the landing `entry` on every snapshot, because `utm_term` is never persisted. The receipt's `explain.contextual` lists each seed driver with its weight, contribution and any dampening; at the hinge it still shows the campaign's contribution applied (+0.066 on the launch offer, +0.060 on the hero: the 0.2 seed times each slot's campaign weight), outscored. The platform seed reads the OS family from `Sec-CH-UA-Platform`, then the user agent, nothing finer; the fired seed (ios or android) is recorded on the decision's receipt as a driver like every other seed, which is what makes it explainable (`src/services/platform.ts`, 12 tests).

- **The arrival.** The landing URL carries `utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=paid-social&utm_term=iphone-18-pro`. The snapshot's `entry` sets the cell's channel and fires the slot's `seeds` (`decide.ts:218-255`): `campaign_term` reads `utm_term` and lifts the launch promotion tagged with it. The first paint answers the ad. The channel is fixed per visit (a 30-minute gap starts a new one), which is fine for a demo that runs in one sitting.
- **The hinge** is arithmetic: as she browses Android, her own brand and OS affinity outgrows the seed, and the Android promotions overtake the lifted one. The seed weight is tuned by measurement so the handoff lands within three or four presses; the receipt action shows `explain.contextual` (the seed) beside the drivers, and the panel reads "campaign context superseded by observed behaviour".
- **The exit** (ruled 2026-09-30 after L1's measurements). The real alarm exists in `ShopperReflex` behind `REFLEX_HOST=do`, but on the object host the ledger consumer stalls after a burst of 20–40 snapshots (receipts pending, the report frozen until a restart; likely `sessionAuthority.ts:178`), while the session host consumes the same load in full. Thresholds stay at the shipped 0.60 / 0.45; beat 3 carries three Samsung touches so brand reaches about 0.625 at beat 4 and crosses 0.45 about 43 seconds after the last press. **Measured (L4):** the alarm on the object host fires 0.3–1.2 s after the closed-form crossing and the frame is `personalization_update` with `source: reflex_alarm` and `affinity.changed[]` (for example `exit brand_samsung_affinity 0.449, thetaOut 0.45`); but the deals module does **not** reorder on the exit: the Samsung offers fall (0.376 → 0.328, 0.307 → 0.262) and still lead the Pixel and razr offers at 0.172, because the Android lean holds. The beat therefore shows the meter, the receipts and the diff row ("Samsung claim withdrawn; Android holds; order unchanged because ecosystem still carries it"), and the phones rail if it moves. Never promise a reorder.

## 6. The honesty ledger

| Real, say so | Representative, label it | Never |
|---|---|---|
| The affinity arithmetic, the gate, the cap, pins, windows, `eligibleWhen`, the receipts, versioned publishes with history and rollback, the census figures, the edge region, the day report as attribution | Regional device sales (synthetic, swap-to-warehouse); the two rehearsed regions; simulated visitors | A lift or revenue number of ours; a bandit that declares a winner; "under 200 ms" (say tens for a known shopper, 235 at the median for a stranger); "no limit"; any other customer's name |

**What Verizon already runs, and therefore what we must be different at.** Their public page code and public record show Adobe (AEP, AJO, CJA, Target) with a signed-in redirect to a personalised homepage, Pega's Customer Decision Hub as the next-best-action engine across channels, Salesforce lead management, Evolv experiments, 15gifts guided selling and a Google Cloud partnership announced 24 August 2026 (both briefs; inference marked there). Everything public is built around the known customer. So the demo never says "you don't personalise today". Its lane is **the moment before identity**: the anonymous stranger served in-session from the first paint; one decision that holds together across the homepage and the phone page (their own pages showed the same bundle at two prices and a $200 message beside a $300 pop-up that day); a receipt behind every card saying why, what it requires and what ends the credit; the merchandiser's pin, weight and rule visible as overrides; and the in-session profile handed to their systems at sign-in, not competing with them.

**Lines a carrier makes non-negotiable, said on stage before anyone asks:**

- We never vary price, discount, financing terms or credit by where someone lives or what a neighbourhood earns. The only geography rule is availability: an offer for a service appears only where that service exists (the home bundle in a covered region). We curate what is around the buy box; the buy box is off limits and the receipt says so.
- Nothing here touches a credit decision. Device financing is theirs, unchanged; the soft credit check behind "$0 down if you qualify" is never shown or implied, and ecosystem, income and geography never decide who sees financing.
- We never pitch "more upgrades": Verizon's upgrade rate fell to 2.6 percent in Q2 2026 as it pulled subsidies, by its own choice. The metrics we show moving are attach, plan and bundle mix, lines per order and module engagement.
- For the anonymous story no customer account data is touched. Where a recognised customer's plan, lines or usage would drive an offer, that is customer proprietary network information under 47 U.S.C. §222 and follows their opt-out; the demo labels any such fact as what their account API would pass.
- The census figure is a neighbourhood aggregate, never this visitor's income.
- On-site behaviour is first-party data they already own; no account data is used for the anonymous story. Where a recognised customer's facts appear (upgrade eligibility), they are labelled as what their account API would pass.
- No protected class, and no ZIP as a proxy for one. Geography never down-ranks a neighbourhood, and the only thing it withholds is an offer for a service that does not exist there. The FCC's digital-discrimination rule was vacated in May 2026 but its statute stands; Verizon's $46.9M location-data fine was upheld in 2025; and Broadband Facts labels put every plan's price and fees in a standard label at every point of sale, so a price that varied by visitor would clash with a federal label.
- **No games.** Their CEO framed the 2026 reset as "one plan, one price, no games". So: no hidden discount for the hesitant shopper, no free-phone bait, no network-superiority copy, and nothing on the prospect site that implies use of subscriber network data.
- **Complement, never critique.** The closing line is the warm hand-off: at sign-in, the session's signals (lines, storage, switcher, market) travel to their decisioning as context, and the page states what was carried and that nothing personal was used before sign-in.
