Any design that personalises from behaviour answers five questions. With individual events as the state, each answer is usually its own rule, and well-kept rules work. We wanted one piece of arithmetic for all five, so our unit of state is the dimension value (brand = samsung): events update it, decisions read it.

## 1. What is remembered?

Not products, not flags: a graded interest per dimension value, a = R/(R + K), rising with each touch and fading on its dimension's horizon. Membership starts at 0.60 and ends only below 0.45, so it does not flicker. One click on a Galaxy S26 card touches four values at once: phones, samsung, android and its price band.

## 2. How is a product chosen?

Each eligible candidate's score dots its tags with the vector, weighted by the slot; the top N pass its rules and a diversity cap. Nothing is set per product; 10,000 seven-tag candidates take about 70,000 multiply-adds. It does not collapse to Samsung: those views built android interest too, shared by every Android offer.

## 3. What happens when nothing has happened yet?

A prior: the region's sales (synthetic in the demo), whose weight λ = k/(k + Σa) is 1 for a stranger and gives way as her interest Σa grows. Her OS family and the ad's campaign term add seeds on arrival. The first paint already answers: with an asserted New York prior and the iPhone ad, the launch offer leads at 0.671*.

## 4. What happens over time?

In the room's compressed memory, brand fades within a minute; ecosystem holds for days. Re-asked under a minute later, brand was 0.398*, below its exit line, with no further event. Learning what works accumulates from served decisions and outcomes behind a dial, γ = 0: it moves nothing until the dial moves.

## 5. How do you know why?

The score is a sum; the receipt writes it out: the leading terms as interest × weight, the prior's part (0.55* of the launch offer's score), what each seed added, any rule applied, the versions in force. The knobs are those numbers, published and versioned. So we can expose them: moving one changes a term on the receipt.

**What a catalog needs.** Attributes in the registry's published vocabulary (brand: apple, samsung, google, motorola), so a candidate and an event land on the same coordinates. Events from the site that carry the product id, and nothing set per product. Formulas in full: the technical note, "How the Vantage signal system works".
