### The beats as data: `public/vantage/beats.json`

**The name.** `public/vantage/beats.json` sits at the Vantage root, beside `beats/`, not inside it. Its `about` says it is the director's script and not a published beat document; those stay `beats/*.json`, which the seed re-anchors and the worker applies by name.

**The 22 presses.** The brief says sixteen beats, but the director has always listed 22 presses of the fifteen beats (1–8, 9a–b, 10a–d, 11–13, 14a–b, 15a–c), and all 22 are data. Each is `{ key, no, act, who, label, declare[], acts[], will[], line, after?, target?, run[] }`, plus two flags:
- `arrives`: the beat mints its own visitor;
- `engineNote`: the exit beat keeps the engine's own note.

**The vocabulary as used.** `click` 33, `wait` 26, `focus` 18, `open` 11, `ask` 8, `assert` 7, `publish` 4, `select` 4, `call` 3, `template` 1. Vantage never needs `custom` (every one of its events comes from a control).

**It grew three verbs, as the brief allows**, each of them a kit capability rather than Vantage code:
- `publish`: a named beat document applied by the worker, or `reset`;
- `ask`: the engine asked again, for a page or for the day's report;
- `template`: the CMS's template for a page, which slot sits where.

`open` also takes `back` (the browser's own Back) and `land: true` (an arrival, not a press).

**The grammar.** `when`/`unless` (one fact), `else`, `do`, `each`, and `note: [verb, what, detail]`. Reusable `places` (the phone by its rail card or the grid; back to it by the browser's Back) take arguments. Strings are templates over `facts()`:
- `{{path}}`, `{{a || b || 'text'}}`;
- sections `{{#…}}` and `{{^…}}`;
- a lone `{{…}}` yields the value itself, so an action's attributes can come whole.

**The escape hatch (`call`), two beats:**
- **Beat 1, `arrive`.** The arrival clears the page's own state, writes the ad's landing into the address bar, mints the stranger from it and reads her region (the override, or the request's). That is Vantage's own state and its arrival rules. The reset, the Arrival tab and the landing around it are verbs.
- **Beat 5, `readMetersBefore` and `askAgainAfterTheMinute`.** Reading her meters before and after the minute and writing the sentence that names the withdrawn claim and the lean that held is the vertical's analysis of what decayed. The visible countdown between them is the kit's `wait { seconds, timer }`, and it honours `?wait=`.

**Checked three ways:**
- The grammar: every step names one known verb, every place and verb exists, and every template renders.
- The text: all 203 literal phrases of the original beats' label, declare, will, line and after are in the same beat of `beats.json`, and every phrase in `beats.json` comes from the original. This was checked with a parser.
- The suites: the 22 director's sentences, outcomes and legibility checks.
