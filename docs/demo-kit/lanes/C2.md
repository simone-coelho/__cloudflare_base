4. **Per-press checks read the screen** where they can: for the 22 per-press checks, prefer the DOM over the kit's state object; where only the state is available, name it in the report.
5. **The request-level suite's source checks** cover tokens in every kit module the page imports (K2 widened them to the imported modules; confirm a planted token in `public/demo-kit/compare.js`, in a scratchpad copy, fails the suite).

### The four assertions, now failing for what they name

Its ink is the share of samples that are not its own commonest colour: about 0.18 to 0.44 on these pages, and 0 for a blank frame. The threshold is 0.05. Where the two pages provably differ, the frames must also differ in at least 1% of samples. Five checks read this:
   - beat 1's Compare: ink and difference;
   - Side by side: the same two frames as the Seam, with ink and difference;

   - both phone-page tools: ink;
