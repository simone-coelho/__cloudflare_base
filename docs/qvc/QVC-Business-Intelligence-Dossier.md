### Easy Pay — mechanics and measured attach rate

**Mechanics** (QVC Easy Pay FAQ + pricing glossary): item price **+ S&H + sales tax** divided across installments; **first installment bills on ship date**, then every ~30–31 days; billing dates set by ship date and **cannot be changed**; **0% interest, no QVC fees**; payable by QCard, major credit card or PayPal, only the first installment authorized at order time. **Subject to QVC approval** (item type, price, payment history; possible soft-inquiry consumer report). Excluded: prior purchases, combining with other EP offers or QCard Special Financing, employee/outlet stores, subsequent Auto-Ship deliveries, gift cards, HSN+/QVC+. **Speed Buy auto-applies Easy Pay**, defaulting to 3 payments with a QCard even on otherwise-ineligible items.

**Measured across 967 live on-air products (2026-08-18):**

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| **Easy Pay attached** | **963 / 967 = 99.6%** |
| Installment distribution | **3 payments: 432 · 2: 420 · 4: 86 · 5: 25** |
| **Minimum price** | **None.** Lowest EP item **$9.90**, minimum installment **$2.18** |
| Attach by band | 99–100% in every band from $0 to $500+ |
| Max EP item | **$3,744.00** |

Their claim **"Easy Pay® Every Day. On Everything."** is close to literally true. ⚠️ **No published Easy Pay lift on AOV or conversion exists.** The 10-K discusses Easy Pay only in a CFPB/BNPL regulatory context. **Do not let anyone put a number on this.**
