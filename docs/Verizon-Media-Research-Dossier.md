## 0. What the 2026-08-07 customer meeting added

- Verizon's core want: **test the ads themselves** — creative, copy, CTA, media format — knowing the platforms are a black box that gives little visibility into what happened.
- The email-testing asymmetry, named precisely: email gives (1) **assignment control** (you choose who receives A vs B), (2) **identity** (every recipient known), (3) **outcome visibility** (your pixel, your landing, your data). Media removes all three.
- Idea on the table from the meeting: carry context in the ad URL → the edge receives it → treat each click as an anonymous individual → trigger/record experiment context at the seam ("like forced bucketing").
- Their four use-case categories stand (brief §2): optimization strategy, audience targeting, creative, geo experimentation.

## 1. Findings that change the story (executive summary)

1. **Every major platform ships person-level randomized split testing** (Meta A/B Test, TikTok Split Test, Google & Microsoft cookie-based experiments, LinkedIn A/B). Assignment is a solved problem *on their side*. What none of them ship: user-level assignment export or advertiser-verifiable measurement — aggregate, platform-attributed readouts only.
2. **The bridge is documented and universal:** every platform lets the ad's identity ride the click URL (macro systems + auto click IDs). So *the arms of a platform split test can carry their cell identity into our edge*. Platform randomizes → URL declares → edge captures → warehouse judges. That is the email test rebuilt for media.
3. **Divergent delivery is real, measured, and incurable in-platform** — even within randomized split-test cells the delivery algorithm chooses who is actually exposed (Braun & Schwartz 2025; Burtch/Moakler/Gordon et al. 2025, *with Meta co-authors*). Platform split tests answer "which ad performs best **as delivered**," not "which message resonates." Clean message-per-audience reads exist only where allocation is controlled — owned properties. This is now citable science, not intuition.
4. **ODP already ships the audience egress we assumed we would build:** Google Audience Sync, Facebook Segment Sync, and TikTok Audience Sync are documented App Directory integrations. The genuinely new build narrows to the **value-scored conversion rail** (CAPI-class APIs + the consent gate). Update brief §7 accordingly.
5. **The edge-set cookie is an ITP answer client-side tools don't have:** Safari caps JS-set cookies to **24 hours** on link-decorated (ad-click) landings — exactly the traffic in question. Server-set first-party cookies (what the worker does at first paint) are the documented mitigation; Optimizely's own docs recommend setting the ID at the CDN/server.
6. **Prefetch is documented traffic pollution:** Meta pre-downloads landing HTML before any tap; TikTok documents the same and says server hits will exceed clicks. The edge can filter these server-side before they contaminate assignment or counts; client-side tags cannot reliably.
7. **Chrome keeps third-party cookies and is dismantling Privacy Sandbox** (Attribution Reporting API et al. deprecated Chrome 144, Jan 2026; removal ~Chrome 150). The industry's aggregate measurement rails are being removed. First-party, server-side capture is the direction of travel, not a vendor preference.
8. ⚠️ **Verizon appears on Instapage's personalization page as a testimonial customer** — they likely already run ad-to-page matching via pre-built pages. Differentiation must be request-time composition vs page-per-ad, behavioral affinity depth, and independent measurement — not the concept of message match. VERIFY the relationship before the pitch.

## 2. Platform experiment tooling (assignment layer)

**Google Ads — custom experiments** (Search/Display/Video/Hotel; not App/Shopping; PMax has its own types)
- Two randomization units, advertiser-selectable: **cookie-based** (recommended; a user sees only one arm) or **search-based** (re-randomized per query). https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6261395
- Advertiser picks the split % (50% recommended), locked at start; base-campaign changes do NOT sync to the experiment arm (drift risk). https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10683687
- Results: campaign-level metrics vs original, significance asterisk ~95%, CI default 80%. Aggregate only; no user-level export. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6318747
- **Ad variations** (RSA find-and-replace tests) still live. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7438541 · **Video experiments** up to 10 arms. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10436762 · **Demand Gen A/B** (traffic split, up to 10 arms). https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13719071 · **PMax experiments** (uplift; PMax-vs-Shopping). https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/12997711
- **Ad rotation "Do not optimize" still exists** but Smart Bidding overrides it, and even rotation = even *auction entry*, not even serving. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/112876

**Meta A/B Test**
- Unit = person; audience divided into "random, non-overlapping groups" before delivery; up to 5 variants. Meta itself says informal on/off comparisons "aren't true experimentation." https://www.facebook.com/business/measurement/ab-testing
- Each arm carries its own budget; UI shows estimated test power (target ≥80%); run ≥7 days, max 30; winner = lowest cost-per-result with simulation-derived confidence (>75% treated as actionable). https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/help/290009911394576 · https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/help/1376548572415613
- Aggregate, Meta-attributed results only.

**TikTok Split Test**
- Two equal non-overlapping groups; each sees one ad group only. Winner at 90% confidence; budget sized to ≥80% power (UI calculator); ≥7 days, max 30. https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/split-testing · https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/split-test-best-practices

**LinkedIn A/B (GA 2023)**
- Member-level assignment, but LinkedIn concedes some members can appear in both groups ("generally insignificant"). https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a529753
- Constraints: max 90 days; ≥3 weeks and ≥$5,000 recommended; validation requires **≥1,000,000 audience per variant**; budgets proportional to weights; group Dynamic Budget Optimization off. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/integrations/experimentation/ab-testing/ab-testing

**Microsoft Advertising experiments**
- Google-style: **CookieBased** (user pinned to an arm) or **TrafficBased** (per-search) splits; 50% recommended. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/experiment?view=bingads-13

**Cross-platform pattern (the load-bearing sentence):** every native tool reports only aggregate, platform-attributed metrics per arm; none exports user-level assignment — so joining test arms to the advertiser's own conversion/revenue data is impossible **unless the arms point at distinguishable landing URLs/parameters**. That URL distinguishability is exactly what we exploit.

## 3. Divergent delivery — the statistics

- **Eckles, Gordon & Johnson (PNAS 2018):** platform "experiments" comparing ad versions don't randomize users; the delivery algorithm creates unintended variance across audiences, confounding creative effects. https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1805363115
- **Braun & Schwartz (Journal of Marketing 2025) — "divergent delivery":** even in platform split tests, *within each randomized cell* the algorithm chooses who is exposed; the "winning" ad may win because it was shown to more responsive users. What you learn is which creative performs best "under this platform's targeting, as delivered" — a policy/total effect, not a creative effect. https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222429241275886 (summary: https://www.ama.org/2025/01/07/can-you-trust-your-ad-data-a-new-study-exposes-a-hidden-flaw-in-a-b-testing-on-digital-ad-platforms/)
- **Burtch, Moakler, Gordon, Zhang, Hill (arXiv 2508.21251, Aug 2025; Meta co-authors):** across 3,204 Lift tests and 181,890 A/B tests on Meta — Lift tests show no meaningful audience imbalance; A/B tests show clear imbalance; divergent delivery is "specific to A/B tests and intentional"; configuration can reduce but never eliminate it. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21251
- **Estimand guidance for our measurement plane:** under person-level randomization into cells, **cell-level ratios are clean** — revenue per impression, revenue per dollar, conversions per 1,000 impressions (platform supplies the denominators per cell; we supply the numerators). Per-click metrics (CVR per click) are conditioned on clicking and are **quality diagnostics**, not causal creative claims. Positioning: "as delivered" is the decision-relevant quantity for budget allocation; message-resonance questions belong onsite where allocation is controlled.

## 4. What crosses the click (signal inventory)

**URL macro systems (what CAN be passed at click time)**
- **Google ValueTrack:** {campaignid}, {adgroupid}, {creative} (ad ID), {feeditemid}, {assetgroupid} (PMax), {keyword} (bid keyword, NOT raw query; blank for DSA/PMax), {matchtype}, {targetid} (criterion incl. **aud-** audience-list IDs, concatenated like `kwd-123:aud-456`), {device}, {network}, {placement} (Display/Video), {loc_physical_ms}/{loc_interest_ms} (geo criterion IDs, offline-resolvable), conditionals {ifmobile:}/{ifsearch:}, Shopping/Hotel params, custom {_params}. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2375447 · https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6305348
- **Meta:** exactly eight tokens — {{campaign.id}}, {{adset.id}}, {{ad.id}}, the three .name equivalents, {{placement}}, {{site_source_name}} (fb/ig/an/msg). No device/geo/audience tokens. (Official spec login-walled; convergent secondaries.) https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2360940870872492
- **TikTok:** __CAMPAIGN_ID__, __AID__ (ad group), __CID__ (creative), name variants, __PLACEMENT__ (TikTok/Pangle), Smart+ __ADID_V2__. No device/geo. https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/track-offsite-web-events-with-utm-parameters
- **LinkedIn:** account/campaign-group/campaign/creative IDs+names only (renamed ACCOUNT/CAMPAIGN/AD_SET/AD from Oct 2025). Thinnest of the majors. https://www.linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a5968064
- **Microsoft:** ValueTrack clone — {CampaignId}, {AdGroupId}, {AdId}, {TargetId} (incl. aud-), {MatchType}, {Network}, {Device}, conditionals — plus ⚠️ **{QueryString} = the raw search query** (the one signal Google won't pass; VERIFY current support). https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/guides/url-tracking-upgraded-urls?view=bingads-13
- **Reddit:** campaign/adgroup/ad IDs+names, {{CLICK_ID}}, **user country (ISO-2) and device type** macros (unusual), cachebuster. (Official page errored; secondaries.) · **Snap:** campaign/adSet/ad/creative id+name macros per most sources — ⚠️ one source claims no dynamic macros; VERIFY in Ads Manager. · **Pinterest:** {campaignid}, {adgroupid}, {adid}, {device}, Shopping params. https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/third-party-and-dynamic-tracking

**Auto-appended click IDs (the conversion-API match keys)**
- **gclid** (auto-tagging default-on) → GA linking, offline conversion upload, store visits; opaque, only Google can resolve. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3095550 · **gbraid/wbraid** (iOS/ATT): aggregate/modeled, not user-level; web landings see wbraid; accepted in uploads; no custom variables with them. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10417364
- **fbclid** → freeze into `_fbc` first-party cookie (fb.1.{ts}.{fbclid}, ~90-day) = CAPI match key. Appears on organic links too — presence ≠ paid click. https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/parameters/fbp-and-fbc
- **ttclid** (auto; don't add manually) → Events API attribution + audience building. https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-click-id
- **msclkid** (auto-tagging default-on) → required for offline conversion goals. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/msa-help/hlp_ba_proc_microsoftclickid
- **li_fat_id** (with enhanced conversion tracking) → LinkedIn CAPI match idType. ⚠️ member-scoped vs click-scoped undocumented. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/conversions/enabling-first-party-cookies?view=li-lms-2026-07
- **rdt_cid** (Reddit), **ScCid** (Snap), **epik** (Pinterest), **twclid** (X) — each the match key for its platform's conversion API.
- All are **one-shot session bridges**, not durable user keys (upload windows 7–90 days).

**Structurally NOT available at click time** (confirmed by absence from official macro lists)
- Which audience/segment matched (partial exception: Google/Microsoft {targetid} aud- on Search/Display; never PMax/Demand Gen; Meta Advantage+ exposes nothing beyond ad-set identity).
- Auction/bid data (no bid, CPC, rank, or position macro anywhere current).
- Impression context and frequency; **view-through exposure is definitionally invisible** (no click → no landing request).
- Raw search query on Google ({keyword} = bid keyword; blank on PMax/DSA). Black-box campaign types (PMax, Demand Gen, Advantage+) systematically reduce the signal.

**Privacy/browser constraints (2026-08)**
- **Safari ITP:** JS cookies capped 24h on navigations from tracker-classified domains (Google/Meta qualify) **with any query string** — i.e., every decorated ad click; script-writable storage ~7 days of Safari use without site interaction; CNAME-cloaked cookies 7 days. Server-set HTTP cookies are the mitigation. https://webkit.org/blog/9521/intelligent-tracking-prevention-2-3/ · https://webkit.org/tracking-prevention/
- **Apple Link Tracking Protection (iOS 17+):** strips known click IDs (gclid, fbclid, twclid, mc_eid…) in **Mail/Messages/Private Browsing only**; normal Safari unaffected; **UTMs untouched**. https://knak.com/blog/link-tracking-updates-ios-17/
- **Chrome:** third-party cookies stay (Apr 2025 decision); **Privacy Sandbox APIs (incl. Attribution Reporting) deprecated Chrome 144 (Jan 2026), removal ~Chrome 150**. Click-ID + first-party-cookie plumbing keeps working; the aggregate replacement rails are being removed. https://privacysandbox.google.com/blog/privacy-sandbox-next-steps · https://www.adexchanger.com/privacy/google-pulls-the-plug-on-topics-paapi-and-other-major-privacy-sandbox-apis-as-the-cma-says-cheerio/
- **Firefox ETP Strict / Brave** strip known tracking params (Meta moved fbclid into the URL fragment in response).

## 5. Landing-side mechanics: identity, policy, data quality

- **Assignment persistence for anonymous visitors:** deterministic hash of a first-party visitor ID (Optimizely Web: `optimizelyEndUserId` + MurmurHash). https://docs.developers.optimizely.com/web-experimentation/docs/how-bucketing-works — **ITP-proofing is server/edge-set cookies or bring-your-own-ID**, per Optimizely's own guidance. https://support.optimizely.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410284274573
- **Same-URL edge assignment is the canonical CDN pattern** (Cloudflare's official example: cookie assignment + internal rewrite, URL never changes). https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/examples/ab-testing/ — nearest paid-landing prior art: Opendoor on Workers (2020), query-param-keyed variants. https://medium.com/opendoor-labs/cloudflare-workers-opendoor-landing-page-infrastructure-824853a34551
- **Google Ads destination policy:** redirects from the final URL to a different domain = Destination Mismatch violation (July 2026 update allows narrow pre-approved cross-domain cases). Same-domain redirects/trackers acceptable; same-URL composition avoids the whole policy surface. https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6368661 · https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/16428020
- **Cloaking vs testing:** Googlebot must see what users see, BUT policy explicitly permits "slightly different content to different people" (offers, language, geo) as long as the promoted product/service is the same — the official basis on which landing personalization/testing is allowed. https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15938075 · Search testing guidance (same-URL variation fine; redirect tests = 302 + canonical). https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/website-testing
- **Ad Rank prices the landing:** quality assessment includes "the expectations users have about your landing page based on the clicked ad creative"; higher-quality ads often pay lower CPCs. Quality Score itself is now framed as a diagnostic, not an auction input — cite Ad Rank for the cost claim. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/1722122 · https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167118
- **Prefetch/bot pollution:** Meta pre-downloads mobile ad landing HTML pre-tap (2016 announcement; trackers filter via `X-Purpose: preview` header ⚠️ vendor-reported). https://www.marketingdive.com/news/facebook-will-pre-load-mobile-content-warns-advertisers-to-optimize-their/425655/ — TikTok prefetch is official and current: "Server request access volume will be higher than clicks"; rep-gated opt-out. https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-prefetch-landing-page — Meta crawlers also hit landing URLs (facebookexternalhit, meta-externalagent). https://developers.facebook.com/docs/sharing/webmasters/web-crawlers
- **SRM taxonomy** (Fabijan et al., KDD 2019): assignment-stage, execution-stage (redirects in one variant!), log-processing, analysis-stage. Paid-specific culprits: unfiltered bots, redirect abandonment, mid-test allocation changes. https://exp-platform.com/Documents/2019_KDDFabijanGupchupFuptaOmhoverVermeerDmitriev.pdf
- **In-app webviews (Meta/Instagram/TikTok):** separate cookie jars per app ecosystem; same human = different visitor in-app vs Safari; referrer often stripped; app-injected JS documented (Krause 2022). Consequence: assignment inconsistency + inflated uniques; mitigations: click-ID session bridging + CAPI-side dedup. https://krausefx.com/blog/ios-privacy-instagram-and-facebook-can-track-anything-you-do-on-any-website-in-their-in-app-browser

## 6. The feed rail: conversion intake + value-based bidding

**Intake per platform (match keys · value · dedup · windows)**
- **Meta CAPI:** hashed em/ph/name/geo, external_id, client IP/UA, fbc/fbp, lead_id; `value`+`currency`; dedup by event_name+event_id within 48h; `event_time` ≤7 days old **or the request fails**; batches ~1,000, one invalid event rejects the batch. Default attribution now 7d click (link clicks only) + 1d engage-through + 1d view (Jan 2026 change). CAPI Gateway = managed AWS stack (~$100/mo/env), lower EMQ ceiling than direct. https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/parameters/server-event/ · https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/conversions-api/best-practices/
- **Google:** Enhanced Conversions web (hashed PII on online conversions; API adjustments keyed on order_id **within 24h**); EC for Leads (hashed email/phone; >63 days after click dropped); classic OCI (gclid/gbraid/wbraid or up to 5 identifiers; value+currency; 90-day window; ~3h to reporting; 2,000/request). **Data Manager API is the go-forward single endpoint (GA Dec 9 2025) — legacy Google Ads API OCI/ECL uploads blocked for developer tokens without prior usage from June 15, 2026. Build on Data Manager from day one.** Hash normalization is the classic silent killer (lowercase/trim, E.164, gmail dot-stripping, then SHA-256). https://developers.google.com/data-manager/api · https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/conversions/upload-offline · https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13258081
- **TikTok Events API:** one API for web/app/offline; hashed email/phone, ttclid, ttp, IP+UA, external_id; value+currency; dedup via shared event_id (5min–48h); 1,000/batch, one bad row fails the batch. https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/events-api · https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/event-deduplication
- **LinkedIn CAPI:** SHA256_EMAIL and/or li_fat_id (recommended together), IP, GAID, name+company; conversionValue{currencyCode,amount}; conversionHappenedAt ≤90 days; ingestion ≤24h + reporting ≤48h; batch 5,000; eventId dedup with Insight Tag. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/linkedin/marketing/integrations/ads-reporting/conversions-api?view=li-lms-2026-07
- **Reddit CAPI:** click_id (rdt_cid) or hashed email or external_id or IP+UA; value+currency; conversion_id dedup; events ≤7 days old. https://hightouch.com/docs/destinations/reddit-ads
- **Microsoft:** msclkid + ConversionName + Time (+ value/currency); ≤90 days after click; 1,000/request; newer Enhanced Conversions + UET CAPI. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/advertising/campaign-management-service/applyofflineconversions?view=bingads-13

**Value-based bidding requirements + evidence**
- **Google tROAS:** ≥15 conversions/30d; report ≥2 distinct values (revenue or **proxy values like lead scores — officially sanctioned**); 3 weeks of values before activating; upload daily, delays <7d or ramp takes months. Evidence: tCPA→tROAS median **+14% conversion value** (Google); EC uplift ~5% Search / 17% YouTube. https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15099424 · https://business.google.com/uk/resources/articles/increase-your-roi-with-value-based-bidding/
- **Meta value optimization:** ≥30 attributed purchase conversions with **≥5 distinct values** /14d; CAPI evidence: 13% lower cost/result (2022), **17.8% lower cost/result avg (Apr 2026 Meta claim via ppc.land)**. pLTV passed as `value` on Purchase = the pLTV-bidding mechanism; ⚠️ "pLTV optimization" as GA named product = third-party reported only, VERIFY with Meta. https://en-gb.facebook.com/business/help/571188993373447 · https://ppc.land/meta-upgrades-pixel-and-conversions-api-to-close-the-gap-for-small-advertisers/
- **TikTok VBO:** web ≥20 Complete Payment events with value /7d; Highest Value or Min ROAS bidding; ~50 conversions + ≥7d learning. Cases: My Medic 2.5x ROAS; Pixel+Events API +19% events / 15% CPA improvement. https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/value-based-optimization-web
- **pLTV practice:** Google explicitly accepts proxy/scored values; Voyantis-style cases (Notion +38% pROAS, Miro +33%, Rappi +30% — vendor-published, directional). https://business.google.com/us/accelerate/resources/articles/google-ads-ai-essentials-values-best-practices/
- **Failure modes:** hash normalization (zero match, no error); dedup mismatch = double counting; one bad row kills the batch (Meta/TikTok); window mismatches vs CRM truth; **value-integrity feedback loop — the bidder optimizes whatever value you send; stale proxies poison delivery** (Optmyzr). Value variance required (≥2 Google / ≥5 Meta distinct values) — flat values give the auction nothing.
- **Meta EMQ:** 0–10 match-quality score; email/phone/click-ID carry most weight; ≥8 great / 6–7.9 good (community benchmarks). https://www.facebook.com/business/help/765081237991954

## 11. Design implications carried into the proposal

1. **Three-tier ad measurement:** (T1) platform split test randomizes, URL carries the cell, edge captures, warehouse judges — causal at cell grain (revenue/impression, revenue/$); (T2) always-on per-creative post-click ledger from macro-tagged URLs + daily cost joins — monitoring/allocation, catches clickbait, not causal; (T3) geo experiments for strategy questions (bid strategy, event/value schemes, granular-vs-broad, local media).
2. **Dual-key identity:** the **click** is the experiment unit; the **visitor** (stable, edge-set first-party ID) is the personalization subject. One event stream, two keys; analysis can use either and cluster clicks by visitor.
3. **The edge is the SRM firewall:** filter prefetch (X-Purpose/UA), crawlers, and bots server-side before assignment/counting; stamp arrival-time; enforce same-URL (no redirect-abandonment SRM).
4. **Egress narrowed:** audiences ride existing ODP apps; build = valued-conversion rail (Meta CAPI, Google **Data Manager API**, TikTok Events API, LinkedIn CAPI) + consent/CPNI gate; batch lists + streaming conversions is the correct architecture (real-time lists don't exist anywhere).
5. **Affinity's media job:** ingress dimension seeding from click context (entry-channel dimension already in the v1 registry), value scoring for VBB (continuous scores = natural value variance), suppression + scored retargeting lists, onsite CMAB with affinity context one layer up.
6. **Telecom LTV loop:** click IDs captured server-side at order → warehouse joins acquisition creative ↔ realized LTV/churn → pLTV values back through the conversion rail. Subscription-grade value bidding no pixel stack can do.
