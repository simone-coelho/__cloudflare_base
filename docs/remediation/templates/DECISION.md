# Decision record — <D01–D10 or explicitly registered additional ID>

Template, not an approval. Retain superseded decisions and link the replacement.

The version-1 tracker records the current question as `pending` or `approved`. `approved` means the accountable person actually selected the recorded outcome; that outcome may be a refusal or rejection of a proposal. Rejected proposals and superseded decisions stay in immutable records and governance history, not invented tracker status values. While the question remains unresolved, keep it pending; never attach a recommendation as its approval record.

Agents may prepare a recommendation and record an actual user decision. They cannot manufacture customer/privacy/commercial/release approval or treat multiple agent votes as that approval. Do not store credentials or sensitive customer material.

Separate local implementation authorization from cloud mutation, release, scope amendment and business/scientific acceptance. Reuse existing valid authority within its actual scope instead of asking the same permission on every task.
