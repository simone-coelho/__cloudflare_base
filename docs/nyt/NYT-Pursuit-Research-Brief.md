# THE NEW YORK TIMES COMPANY — Pursuit Research Brief
**Window:** mid-2025 → Aug 2026 · **Compiled:** 2026-08-20 · Sources: SEC EDGAR (primary), CourtListener RECAP, nytco.com, NiemanLab, Press Gazette, PPC Land, Motley Fool transcript, Google News RSS corroboration.

**The "why now" (three converging events, all last ~90 days):**
1. **Aug 5, 2026** — On the Q2 call, CEO Meredith Kopit Levien conceded platform traffic decline is hitting NYT itself: "**The Times isn't immune to that impact**." Net digital adds decelerated (310K→280K) and the stock sold off (Reuters, 2026-08-05).
2. **Jun 1, 2026** — Publisher A.G. Sulzberger's WAN-IFRA keynote made "**Be a destination first**… direct relationships… **loyal, habituated and valuable**" the company's survival doctrine.
3. **Apr 13, 2026** — Exec restructure created exactly two buying-centre owners: Alex Hardiman (EVP: product + engineering + **AI product and platforms** + ad product) and Hannah Yang (EVP: subscription growth + marketing + **data**).
They have a live 15M-by-2027 subscriber target requiring ~275K adds/qtr; Q2 delivered 280K — zero margin of safety.

## 2. The AI story

### NYT v. OpenAI/Microsoft — VERIFIED via CourtListener RECAP
Case 1:23-cv-11195 (S.D.N.Y.), consolidated into **MDL 1:25-md-03143, In re: OpenAI Copyright Infringement Litigation** (formed 2025-04-11), Judge Sidney Stein. Docket IDs 68117049 / 69879510.
- 2026-06-25: NYT amended complaint (NYT's own coverage).
- **2026-07-09/10: NYT-led group moved for SANCTIONS against OpenAI** — alleging concealment of **~78M ChatGPT logs** / evidence tampering (Reuters, AP, TechCrunch, Ars Technica, 2026-07-09).
- **2026-08-14: OpenAI filed opposition** (ECF 1459, verified on docket).
- **2026-08-19: Second Amended Complaint filed** (MDL ECF 1676, verified) — case escalating as of yesterday.
- Litigation cost (VERIFIED from filings): 2024 $10.8M + 2025 $13.3M + H1'26 $8.84M = **$32.9M cumulative**.

### NYT v. Perplexity — VERIFIED existence
Filed **2025-12-05** (nytimes.com coverage; also cited by Sulzberger). ⚠️ Claim details not retrieved.

### Amazon licensing deal — VERIFIED existence; terms REPORTED only
Announced **2025-05-29** — NYT's first gen-AI licensing deal; multiyear; covers NYT, Cooking, The Athletic content for Amazon AI including **Alexa+** (Reuters, Axios, CNBC, Verge, nytco). Terms: **WSJ exclusive 2025-07-30: "at least $20M/yr"** (some outlets: up to $25M). Never company-confirmed — cite as reported. Financial trace: 10-K attributes +$14.4M 2025 licensing growth to "commercial agreements with third-party digital platforms". **No Google/Meta/OpenAI licensing deals found for NYT** — they litigate where others license (News Corp–Meta $50M/yr, NY Post 2026-03-04, for contrast).

### Internal AI / "Echo" — VERIFIED
Feb 2025: NYT green-lit AI for editorial/product staff, debuted internal tool **Echo** (Semafor 2025-02-16; NiemanLab 2025-02). Approved tools: GitHub Copilot, Google Vertex AI, NotebookLM, NYT's ChatExplorer, some Amazon AI products, OpenAI non-ChatGPT API with legal approval. Editorial AI guidelines distributed. Custom newsroom AI in production (NiemanLab 2026-02 "manosphere tracker"). **No standalone AI chief — AI product/platforms sits under EVP Alex Hardiman.** New AI beat on Science desk (Cade Metz, 2026-08-12).

### AI Overviews / zero-click — VERIFIED, on the record
**Kopit Levien, Q2'26 call (verbatim):** "…a rapidly changing information ecosystem shaped by a small number of big tech companies whose moves continue to result in **less traffic to publishers. The Times isn't immune to that impact**…" And in Q&A: "we are building resilience… by making our **destination product experiences, I'd say now, especially our apps even more effective at engaging prospects**… our aim… is to **become less reliant on the intermediaries**."
Prepared remarks: https://nytco-assets.nytimes.com/2026/08/Q2-2026-Prepared-Remarks.pdf · NiemanLab writeup 2026-08-05.
10-K risk factor ties ad targeting/cookies erosion to "a decrease in referral traffic from generative AI products."

### Sulzberger keynote — the doctrine (VERIFIED, full text)
"A.I., Journalism and the Uncertain Future of the Public Square," WAN-IFRA, **2026-06-01**: https://www.nytco.com/press/a-i-journalism-and-the-uncertain-future-of-the-public-square/
Key verbatim: "**Be a destination first**… The clearest path to support quality reporting will be through **direct relationships with audiences**… to make them **loyal, habituated and valuable**, your audience must learn it's better to engage with you directly." Also: "Use AI the right way… a shift of this size will **lay waste to any organization that refuses to evolve**"; "A.I. products are likely not just to supplement but **replace direct relationships** with news organizations."

## 5. Advertising / first-party data — VERIFIED

10-K (twice): "solutions that use **proprietary first-party data** to generate predictive insights." Digital = ~73% of ad revenue; mostly direct-sold.
**Concrete cookie-less proof point:** **NYT Advertising's first data clean room collaboration** (Decentriq), campaign Q4 2025, case study Jun 2026 (ppc.land, 2026-06-21): global wealth manager; **+20% Quality Visit Rate, +61% intl CTR lift, +25% US uplift; 1-in-4 of the bank's customers are Times readers**; program renewed. First-ever activation of NYTA first-party audiences in a clean room.
CEO on ads (verbatim): "ad products that really work for marketers. So **campaigns renew because the ads perform**." New **middle-market ad sales team** staffed 2026 (CFO).

## 6. Funnel / pricing / churn — VERIFIED

- Promo mechanics (Press Gazette 2025-02-05): All Access list **$25/mo, intro $2/mo**; news-only purchase path effectively withdrawn (news-only subs 1.93M→1.47M in 2025).
- Price increase: bundle **$25→$30** for tenured cohort (Q1'26, CFO verbatim).
- Churn (10-K verbatim): cancels driven by "subscribers' perception that they **do not engage with our content sufficiently**; **the end of a subscriber's promotional pricing (which is an important aspect of our strategy)**… New subscriber cohorts may not retain at the same rate as prior cohorts."
- S&M +23.6% YoY; CFO: majority of starts still organic, marketing an "additional growth lever."

**Best single quote for the brief — 10-K, "Using technology and data to propel our growth" (verbatim):** 2026 priorities are "strengthening our data management infrastructure, enhancing the platforms that power our multiproduct digital bundle, and advancing machine-learning and AI applications across our business… these investments… help us **better engage, habituate, convert and retain more subscribers**." Their own four-verb funnel, in a public filing.
