# Behavioral Targeting and Intelligence: see it work

For marketing: the engine at work on one sample store, in seven steps to walk through before writing or speaking about it.

## The first page feels local

Morrow & Vale is a sample store invented for this tour, and what visitors in each region lean toward is invented too.

Before any click, the engine knows only the visitor's region. The sections visitors there lean toward come first, and the hero follows the strongest lean.

![The same store opens differently in Washington State and in Florida, and the frames that follow track the visitor in Washington State.](../figures/shot-tour-step-1.png)

## The first click changes the page

A click on a product pours into the bucket for what it touches, such as knitwear. An add to the bag counts three times a look, so that bucket passes its entry line at once.

Two sections trade places on the next page, and the receipt records the click.

![One add to the bag, and Soft knits and Rain-ready layers trade places.](../figures/shot-tour-step-2.png)

## A second interest fills

A look at something different fills a second bucket partway. One look is a clue, not a conclusion.

The page leans on both: the first interest still leads, and the second one's section climbs past the brand's recommendations carousel.

![One look at a linen shirt lifts The linen shop above the recommendations carousel.](../figures/shot-tour-step-3.png)

## The first bucket drains, and the visitor is released

Every bucket drains on its own, and nobody writes an expiration rule. Below its release line, the visitor is released from that interest, its section drops back, and the receipt records the release.

This sample store keeps interest for 5 minutes so the tour can show it fade; each brand sets how long its buckets hold, and the engine's default is 14 days.

![Five minutes on, knitwear has drained below its release line, and Soft knits drops back behind Rain-ready layers.](../figures/shot-tour-step-4.png)

## A pin holds its place

A merchandiser sold the partner spotlight the first place on the home page, so the brand pins it there. The brand's rules come first, and the engine ranks the rest around them.

The pinned section holds its place while the others reorder, and the receipt says ranking never ran for it.

![The pinned partner spotlight holds the first place while Soft knits and Rain-ready layers trade places below it.](../figures/shot-tour-step-5.png)

## Every decision comes with a receipt

The receipt shows what was served, in which place, and the arithmetic behind it, so a person can recompute the page by hand.

Each score blends the visitor's own interest with the region's lean. The less the engine has seen of the visitor, the more the region counts.

![The receipt for the last page: the pin first, then four places, each worked out in plain numbers.](../figures/shot-tour-step-6.png)

## Learning waits for the brand's dial

It records what works from the first day and changes nothing until the brand turns the dial.

A holdout keeps a share of visitors on the site's own page, so the brand measures the lift on its own numbers. The whole story is on [What it is](https://edge-platform-bti.expedge.workers.dev/what-it-is), and every constant has an instrument on [How it works, interactive](https://edge-platform-bti.expedge.workers.dev/how-it-works-interactive).

![The learning dial rests at zero beside what this visit recorded, and a holdout keeps a share of visitors on the site's own page.](../figures/shot-tour-step-7.png)
