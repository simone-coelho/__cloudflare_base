## 1. What the page is

A side-navigation page in the site's chrome (P1's masthead, navigation, reader tag, stamp, title with `#package-name`, the one rule first and last, footer), with the Vantage page's instruments as dark panels. The story comes first, as V2 §2 orders it, then the mechanism:

- **The story.** The problem; what it is (the stylist, with V2's recommendations sentence); what it does, every capability V2 §2.3 lists, each pointing to the section that shows it (Opal, ODP and Feature Experimentation, the shoppers' AI add-ons and identity are stated once each, with no instrument); why customers choose it, the ten reasons, unranked, with "each customer weighs them differently".
- **How it works.** 1 the unit of state (the diagram); 2 interest as a quantity (K, τ, the two lines, the weight, the clock, t*); 3 one view, four touches (the event, the touches, the live visitor); 4 every candidate scored (the deals slot and the phones list, with weights, the cap, a pin, the condition, the 10,000 count); 5 the first page feels local (the regional prior, λ, the seeds, a stranger's receipt, the hinge); 6 two memories; the worked example, end to end; 7 learning behind the dial (p̂, lift, γ, the pooling levels); 8 the three published documents, annotated; 9 the example record, recomputed twice; 10 what a customer provides, with what we use it for.
- **About this page**, in place of the Vantage honesty ledger: what is an example, what is computed, what is an input; the page holds no credential, stores nothing, sends nothing and times nothing. No label, chip or legend anywhere.

Every instrument has a lead paragraph above it (what you are looking at, try, watch for), and every control group and instrument a hint, as on the Vantage page.
