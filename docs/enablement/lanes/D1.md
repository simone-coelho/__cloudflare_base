## 3. Choices made for version 2, and why

1. **Every label, chip, legend and open note is gone**, and so are the words Built, Designed, Open, demo, today and
   stage, in every figure's text, title and description (V2 §1).
2. **No number.** The product codes R2, D1 and KV gave way to plain names: file storage, database, key-value store.
3. **The zones are named for the product,** not for the demo workers. "Kept by each Worker" and "Each deployment's
   own storage" replace "Shared by the demo workers".
4. **Opal sits beside the team, above Cloudflare,** joined to the operator and to nothing in Cloudflare. That
   draws the rule "never in a live decision" instead of writing it twice.
5. **Fir is the engine and light fir is Opal,** in figures 1 and 3 alike.
6. **Feature Experimentation is drawn** beside ODP and the warehouse, because V2 §2.3 names the link. The
   coordinator's list for this figure named ODP, the warehouse, Opal and the published documents.
7. **The published documents have their own box,** in the deployment's storage, with the ledger and the day reports
   named beside them.
8. **Ten steps, with pins reserved before scoring.** Gates and pins are one step, after S2 3.9 to 3.10 and S5 C1
   ("gates, then pins, then ranking"). The diversity cap moved into "Ranking and the cap". All ten boxes are 25 px
   taller so the learning step can carry V2's sentence verbatim.
9. **The consent check stays a step.** It is a capability (S5 A1), stated as one, not a caveat.
10. **"Journey stage" is left out of the ODP arrows,** to keep stage words out; "events and audiences" stands.
11. **"Worker" is capitalized,** as the glossary spells it (review round one's recommendation, now applied).

## 4. Points where the sources differed, and what the figures follow

1. **The AI add-ons.** V2 §1 has customers switch them on with their credits; the pricing guide meters them per use.
   The card says "Credits apply", which holds under both.
2. **Tagging.** V2 §2.3 has Opal tagging content; S2 6.1 describes tagging under the tenant's own model key. V2
   governs, so the tagging card is Opal's.
3. **The answer and the receipt.** The answer arrow carries the reasons, and the full receipt is an output read in
   the console (S2 3.14), as in version 1.

## 5. Capture and measurement

**Tools,** in the session scratchpad (`.../6d912628-2c4c-48d2-9676-33d6a79a76ce/scratchpad/d1/`):

- `fig.mjs` audits and captures;
- `measure.mjs` measured every new line in three fonts before it was drawn;
- `phone.mjs` renders a figure as a phone shows it.

The method follows lane W1's `cdp-capture.mjs`:

- headless Chrome (`--headless=new`), on its own profile in the scratchpad, driven over CDP;
- the viewport set to the viewBox size, the SVG file loaded directly;
- `Page.captureScreenshot` clipped to the canvas at device scale 2;
- `Browser.close` on every run, never a kill.

**The overflow audit,** at native size, on every text element:

- its advance width and its glyph extent (baseline minus 0.76 em to plus 0.24 em) must sit inside the first rect of
  its box, 8 px in from each side and 3 px from top and bottom;
- no two texts may overlap;
- no two boxes may partly overlap;
- nothing may leave the canvas;
- any line with less than 5 % spare width is reported.

It runs in the stack as written (Roboto renders here), then with the root forced to Segoe UI and to Arial; Verdana
runs as a stress test only.

**Results, final files:** all three report 0 overflow, 0 text overlaps, 0 partial box overlaps, 0 off canvas and 0
lines under 5 % spare width, in Roboto, Segoe UI and Arial. Verdana overflows 6 to 12 lines, as expected.

| Smallest text | On the canvas | At 6.5 in in print | At 2x zoom on a phone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Figures 1 and 3 | 19 px | 8.1 pt | 12.4 px |
| Figure 2 (its governing-document footers) | 18 px | 7.7 pt | 11.7 px |

Body text is 20 px (8.5 pt), box titles 21 to 23 px, figure titles 32 px. Pages show the figures in a frame that
scrolls sideways at 720 px or more on a phone (the lead's ruling, §8).

## 6. For the page and Word lanes

- **Pages:** inline the SVG, which works as it is (presentation attributes only, ids prefixed `arch-`, `path-`,
  `bda-`, a `<title>` and a full `<desc>` through `aria-labelledby`), or add `img-src 'self'` for an `<img>`.
- **There is no legend to match.** The only colors that carry meaning are fir for the engine and light fir for Opal.
  The dashed outlines in figure 1 group stores and are named on the figure.
- **The package name is in no figure.**
