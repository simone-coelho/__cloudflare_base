## 3. Where the figures depart from the brief, and why

1. **The published documents are in file storage (R2), not in KV.** The brief listed "the published documents
   store (KV)". S2 puts the publication sets and their revisions in R2 (2.2 `STORAGE`, 4.1, 2.7 arrow 8). KV
   holds the published snapshots of the regional prior and the learned lift (2.2 `CACHE`), drawn as their own box.
2. **R2 is labelled "File storage", not "object storage".** S2 0 gives both names. "Object" already means the
   visitor's objects in the same picture, so the plain one was used.
3. **The answer carries reasons; the receipt is an output to the operator.** The brief's arrow was "a snapshot
   answer with a receipt". S2 3.14: the page's own answer carries each decision's drivers and diversity note; the
   full receipt goes to the ledger and is read in the console. The site box says "each with its reasons".
4. **An arrow for the consent choice is added** (S2 2.7 arrow 2), because nothing is personalized or recorded
   without it.
5. **Two steps of the chain moved.** Identity and session come before consent: the consent choice is bound to
   the visitor's own session grant (S2 3.1, 3.2). The regional prior comes before gating and scoring: the score
   sums the slot's weight times the blended interest (S2 3.6, 3.7, 3.11).
6. **Two steps are governed by no published document.** Identity and session rest on worker settings set by an
   engineer (S2 4.6); consent rests on the visitor's own choice (S2 3.2, 7.7). The footers say so.
7. **Labels are noun phrases.** The brief's clauses became "Graded, fading interest", "Gated candidates" and
   "Each piece's score"; "consent checked" became "The consent check".
8. **Figure 3 has a fourth verdict, "Credits not decided",** for design-time tagging. The guide gives it credits;
   S2 6.3 says whether it is billed as Opal credits is open.
9. **Gender-neutral wording** (the coordinator's ruling): "the visitor", "their", "them". A word-boundary,
   case-insensitive scan of each SVG, comments included, for she, her, hers, he, him and his finds zero matches.

## 5. Capture and measurement

**Tools,** in the session scratchpad (`.../6d912628-2c4c-48d2-9676-33d6a79a76ce/scratchpad/d1/`): `fig.mjs` audits
and captures; `measure.mjs` measured every candidate line in three fonts before it was drawn; `phone.mjs` renders
a figure as a phone shows it. The method follows lane W1's `cdp-capture.mjs` (after lane R2's `cdp-run.mjs`):

- headless Chrome (`C:/Program Files/Google/Chrome/Application/chrome.exe`, `--headless=new`), on its own profile
  in the scratchpad, driven over CDP on a WebSocket;
- `Emulation.setDeviceMetricsOverride` at the viewBox size, the SVG file loaded directly;
- `Page.captureScreenshot` clipped to the canvas at device scale 2;
- Chrome closed with `Browser.close` every run, never killed.

**The overflow audit,** at native size (device scale 1), on every text element:

- its horizontal extent (getBBox) and its glyph extent (baseline minus 0.76 em to plus 0.24 em) must sit inside
  the first rect of its box, 8 px in from each side and 3 px from top and bottom;
- no two texts may overlap;
- no two boxes may partly overlap (nesting is allowed);
- nothing may leave the canvas;
- any line with less than 5 % spare width is reported. Apple devices fall back to a system font a little wider
  than Roboto, and that margin is for them.

It runs with the stack as written (Roboto renders on this machine), then with the root forced to Segoe UI and to
Arial, the Windows and Helvetica-class fallbacks. Verdana, about 10 % wider and not in the stack, runs as a
stress test only.

**Results, final files:** all three report 0 overflow, 0 text overlaps, 0 partial box overlaps, 0 off canvas and 0
lines under 5 % spare width, in Roboto, Segoe UI and Arial. Verdana overflows 11 to 17 lines, which is expected
and does not count.

**On a phone,** a 390 px screen with 16 px gutters shows a figure 358 px wide. There the body renders at 6.5 CSS px

pinch (13 px at 2x) or in landscape, not at fit width. No single drawing this dense serves both 6.5 in print and a
phone at fit width. Two ways close the gap:

- **No new drawing.** A page shows the figure in a frame with `overflow-x: auto` and the SVG at `min-width:
  720px` on narrow screens. The body is then 13.1 px and the smallest text 11.8 px. The page links the SVG file
  for a full-screen view.
- **Stacked phone versions.** The same words, reflowed to a 400 px canvas. They are not drawn in this pass,
  because the Word builds were waiting; this lane can draw them on request, with a text-parity check against the
  print versions.

## 6. For the page and Word lanes

- **Word:** `![caption](../figures/<name>.png)`, alone on a line in a file under `docs/enablement/content/`.
  Suggested captions are the figure titles.
- **Pages:** the site's policy allows `img-src data:` only, so either inline the SVG markup (no policy change) or
  add `img-src 'self'` to `enablement/site/_headers` for an `<img>`.
- **Safe to inline as they are:**
  - presentation attributes only: no `<style>` element, no `style` attribute, no script, no external reference;
  - ids prefixed per figure (`arch-`, `path-`, `bda-`), so all three can share a page;
  - each has a `<title>` and a full `<desc>` through `aria-labelledby`. The `<desc>` can serve as the page's
    text alternative.
- **Size:** set `width: 100%; height: auto`. The `width` and `height` attributes give the native size.
- **One legend, reused by pages:**
  - Built: LF Green `#ABFF44` pill, Dark Fir `#08251A` text;
  - Designed: Light Blue `#91DBDA` pill, Dark Fir text;
  - Open: white pill with a 2 px dashed Dark Fir border (dashes 5 and 4), Dark Fir text.
- **Figure 3's verdict bands:**
  - Dark Fir: "No AI · no credits";
  - Light Fir `#197050`: "Credits apply";
  - Neutral 5 `#C3CEAF`: "Metered per use";
  - white with a dashed border: "Credits not decided".
- **Type:** the site's body stack (Roboto, Segoe UI, system-ui, -apple-system, Helvetica Neue, Arial,
  sans-serif); nothing is fetched.
- **The package name is in no figure.** The figures say "the engine", so a rename needs no figure change.
