1. **S2 has six labels; the brief has three.** Withdrawn becomes "switched off by policy and not offered" on the never-say list; Demo only becomes words in a row; Unverified is never stated (design §4.2).
2. **S2 §5.7's honesty wording, "It records from day one and moves nothing until the dial moves", contradicts the brief's fact that with the shipped SDK nothing is recorded yet** (and S2's own §3.15 and §5.1). Pieces use P4 everywhere and P5 in (c) and (d); "from day one" is on the never-say list.
3. **S2 §1's paragraph "a marketer can repeat"** says each answer comes "with a receipt that shows the arithmetic", opens with the engineering name "content decision service", and says "a merchandiser changes" every setting. P1 carries it with three edits, each justified by S2 itself (§3.14, §3.15, §4.5; design §4.7).
4. **S3's rule sentences.** "ODP is the memory; the edge is the reflex" contradicts the brief and S2 §7.3, and goes on the never-say list. "They score people; we decide pages" describes another system, against the brief, and is not used. "This initiative is about the other 90 percent" rests on a share S2 does not hold, and is not used.
5. **S3's Opal rule, "The engine decides; Opal creates and explains"**, reads as if Opal works with the engine today, while S2 §6.2 and the brief say it is not wired. Kept as the boundary rule, with "Opal is not connected to the engine today." beside it.
6. **S3's anatomy of the credits page** colors every row by credit verdict, which would be a second color legend against the brief's one legend on every page. Credit verdicts become words in their own column.
7. **S3 rule 8, "honesty in one ledger"**, against the brief's "honesty inline": one label on the row it qualifies, never a repeated caveat, with the never-say table as the ledger.
8. **S3 keeps "affinity" as the technical term**; the brief says interest, not affinity. Affinity is deprecated for readers and explained for engineers.
9. **S3 keeps "experience personalization" and "content personalization"** as the names of two activations; S2 does not define them, so they are not used in this release (design §4.8 item 4).
10. **S3 notes the recent documents write British English**; the brief says American. American throughout.
11. **BOOTSTRAP §7 and older material quote timing figures**; the brief forbids any. None is quoted; the cache lifetime is not quoted either (design §4.3).
12. **S1 §2.2 lists demo memories in seconds and minutes** that S2 does not hold. They appear only in the design's list of numbers never to quote as the engine's.
13. **BOOTSTRAP §3 says the Vantage phone page reorders its modules; S2 §10.1 labels module sequencing Designed.** Raised to the lead and settled by S2 §10.4: the module order inside a slot is Built, the homepages' slot placement is the demo page's own template (Demo only), and per-context layout stays Designed.
