## 6. What I could not honor as written, and why

1. **The shot names.** The lead's brief gave `data-shot="shot-se-instruments"`, `"shot-se-privacy"` and
   `"shot-se-asks"`. V3 §5 pairs `data-shot="<name>"` with `shot-<name>.png`, and S1's check fails the prefixed form, so
   the values are `se-instruments`, `se-privacy` and `se-asks`, and the markdown names `shot-se-instruments.png`,
   `shot-se-privacy.png` and `shot-se-asks.png`. Each shot shows its caption inside it.
2. **The prior's constant.** As §3 says: kBlend, written k, never the interest's K.
3. **A pin that may not show.** FACTS §2 and S2 §3.10 record that today's code empties the whole slot when a pinned piece
   is not eligible (S2 §9.4 item 8). V3 §4.5, the glossary ("Pin", "Gate") and review round three's ruling R3-10 (lane
   H1's note) describe the finished product: the pinned piece is simply not shown and the rest of the slot ranks as
   usual. The material describes the finished product, so the page says that.
4. **The ten reasons and the weighing sentence.** V3 §4.5 and mock 03 gave the engineers' page no section for them; the
   lead ruled that they belong here (V2 §5; the design's §9 gate, item 1), and they are now the section after the spine
   (§8).
5. **No "one rule" first and last.** V3 §5 places the spine right after the h1 and the reader line, and the mocks show no
   rule. S1's builder reads a piece's rule only from the reader line or the block after it, so the landing page's card for
   this piece shows none. (The text check's fallback for a card rule reads a markdown's first whole-bold line, which here
   is a card title; it applies only if the landing page shows a rule for this piece.)
6. **Numbers in the new figures.** The design's §3.10 keeps D1's three figures free of numbers. V3 asks for the chain
   "for the engine's defaults" and for "a sample receipt", which need them. Every number in my figures is a default
   constant, arithmetic from one, or the example's invented settings; none is a measurement or a timing.
7. **The quarry's facts not carried.** Part A's story is the spine's now, and its four quotations have no place in V3's
   sections. Not carried from Part B: the registry's 24 values per dimension, the count of work per candidate, the
   vocabulary check on event values, boots at 0.32 on the return, and the call table's request and answer columns (the
   calls themselves are in the integration figure); the lead accepted leaving these out. "An impression weighs nothing"
   returned, on the lead's ruling, as one line in the interest card.
8. **The Word copy is lane W2's.** Each section of the markdown opens with its heading, at most one lead sentence and then
   its picture; each card's picture line comes before the card's title (a bold line), formulas (one code line each),
   worked example and meaning, so the copy can show the picture with the text or without it.
9. **No script of the page's own.** Nothing on it needs one; site.js marks the figure frames that scroll sideways.
