## The page

After the stamped spine, a stepper ("Step n of 7", Back, Next) and one live board: a callout, the storefront of
Morrow & Vale, and beside it the clock, the interest buckets, "What the page leans on" and the receipt panel. One step
shows at a time, its explanation above the board in a few short lines; the callout says what to do, then what
changed, and the store marks what moved (`.mark` elements: moved up, moved down, pinned). On a narrow screen the
buckets sit between the callout and the store and stay in view while the store scrolls, so a click and the bucket it
fills are seen together.

## The storyboard without the script

Each step's figure holds two inline SVG panes (the storefront; the buckets, balance and receipt, or for steps 6 and 7
the whole receipt and the dial with the holdout) and a `<figcaption>`, the caption that is also the markdown's picture
line. Step 1 shows the two regions' first pages side by side; steps 2 to 7 follow the visitor in Washington State and
the caption of step 1 says so. The frames are generated in the scratchpad by `stills.mjs`, which loads the model block
from the served script and draws each frame from the replayed state, and `assemble.mjs` writes them into the page
from a hand-written template, keeping any stamped block as it stands. Each pane has a title and a description that
reads the frame in words. With the script running the frames give way to the live board (class `is-live`); print
shows the storyboard again. **For lane W2:** the `data-shot` elements are the frames, visible with JavaScript off or
under print media, so capture them that way.

## Where this page departs from the letter of a brief, and why

1. **The sample store's memory is 5 minutes, not the 14-day default.** V3 names the control "A few minutes pass", and
   at 14 days a few minutes drain nothing. A brand publishes its memory (glossary "Memory", FACTS §1), so the store
   sets a short one, and step 4's text says so with the engine's default beside it. Every other constant is the
   engine's default.
2. **The first click is an add to the bag.** A release needs an entry first (FACTS §1), and one look gives 0.357,
   below both lines. Only an action weighing 2.7 or more passes the entry line in one click; the engine's shipped add
   to cart weighs 3. So step 2's one click is an add, which both trades two sections and enters, and step 4 can
   release on the engine's arithmetic.
3. **kBlend is 1, the compiled default, where M1 used 0.5.** With the add, kBlend 1 shows every move, and the page
   then matches the engineers' interactive page; this settles review finding L3-5.
4. **Florida's invented leans mirror Washington State's** (linen 1, rain layers 0.75, tableware 0.5, knitwear 0; M1 had
   tableware 0.75, rain layers 0.5), so both regions tell the same seven steps and the sold section is never the
   first interest.
5. **The carousel section is "Recommended picks",** not "Recommended for you". The lead's ruling of 2026-10-03 lets a
   static element quote that title behind `data-ui-label`, as the spine now does, but the check still refuses "you" in
   any string a script can write, and the tour's live store is built by its script. The still frames use the same
   title, so the store reads the same with and without the script.
6. **`data-shot="tour-step-N"`, not `"shot-tour-step-N"`.** S1's check pairs `data-shot="<name>"` with
   `shot-<name>.png`, as V3 §5 does, and names the prefix as the error; the markdown keeps `shot-tour-step-N.png`.
7. **No rule callout, first or last.** V3 puts the spine first inside the article after the h1 and the reader line,
   and the tour has no rule of its own (the landing page reads rules from the three narratives only).
8. **What M1's page held beyond the four scenes is not carried:** the gate (V3's step 5 and the spine's fourth frame
   are the pin), "The stylist has more moves", the ten reasons and the two link cards. V3 §4.2 lists the spine and
   the seven steps; step 7 ends with one line linking What it is and How it works, interactive.
9. **The dial is drawn at zero, not turned.** Turning it is the brand's act, and a lift from this one visit would be
   an invented lift figure. The holdout's share is drawn as one in twenty, the compiled default's 5 percent, said to
   be the brand's setting.
10. **The diversity cap does not appear:** no slot of the sample store has one, and no step calls for it.
11. **Only the card the step asks for is a button;** the other cards are pictures of the store, so the tour cannot
    drift from its seven steps.
12. **The sample store's own color tokens stay in `see-it-work.css`,** not in `spine.css` as V3 §5 asks of a new
    token. They are the store's palette, not the spine's or the site's, scoped to the tour's article, each with its dark
    value (the lead's ruling on M4-8, 2026-10-03).
