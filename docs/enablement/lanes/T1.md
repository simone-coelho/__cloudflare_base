## The page

Above the player only the title, the reader tag and one line: "One visitor, seven moments, on a sample store: press
Show me, or click what is highlighted." Then one frame holds the whole step:

- **The bar**, sticky at the top of the window while the frame is on screen: Back (absent on step 1), "Step n of 7 ·
  title", seven dots, and the step's controls. Step 1: "Pick where this visitor is:" and Washington State and Florida,
  nothing pressed until the reader picks; a pick shows that region's first page and Next appears beside the buttons,
  which stay switchable. Steps 2 to 5: Show me and Next. Step 6: Next. Step 7: Start again.
- **The explanation**: one line of at most two sentences and 45 words (the longest, step 4's, is 44), and the step's
  fixed sentence below it in smaller type: performance in step 2, recommendations in step 3, AI in step 6, learning in
  step 7, each verbatim from its source of record (V2 §3 for performance and learning, `spine.md` for the other two).
- **The board**: the compact store on the left (the hero and five sections, each a title and its three cards on one
  line), and on the right the interest buckets, "What the page leans on" and the receipt. At 1366×768 and 1440×900
  the whole frame, bar to bottom, fits the window less 24 px at every step (727 px at the tallest), all six rows in
  view.
- **The target**: the card the step asks for (at step 4, the "Let five minutes pass" button in the store's head) is
  outlined, pulses three times (never under reduced motion) and carries its callout attached beside it, naming the
  action and what to watch, as `data-reader`.
- **What changed is marked where it changed**: "moved up", "moved down" and "pinned" on the sections; the bucket fills
  or drains in view; the receipt shows the newest event first, its new lines lit, and a polite live region says the
  result and the new lines.

**The controls.** Show me performs the step's action in front of the reader and stays on the step; clicking the
target does the same; Show me then goes. Next is never disabled: pressed before the action, it performs it, lets the
0.7-second glide land, holds 1.2 seconds, then advances (at once under reduced motion); pressed during that hold or
during step 4's drain, it advances at once. Back restores each step as the reader left it, its action done, and
going forward again reopens each completed step done, until the region changes or Start again. Start again returns to
step 1 with no region picked. Every control brings the frame's top to the window's top (instantly under reduced
motion); on a phone an action instead brings the change into view under the bar, as below.

**Focus and announcements.** After a step change focus moves to the bar's primary control (the first region button,
or Next once a region is picked; Show me at steps 2 to 5, or Next once the step is done; Next at step 6; Start again
at step 7). After a pick it stays on the chosen region button; after an action it moves to Next. Tab follows the
visual order: Back, the step's controls, then the target in the store. One polite live region says "Step n of 7:
title." on a step change and the result with "New on the receipt: …" after an action.

**A phone (390×844).** The bar stays at the top; the store sits above the panels. After an action the page scrolls so
the changed section sits under the bar with its bucket and the newest receipt line below it; when everything still
fits, the view starts higher, at the store's top or at a pinned section above the change, so step 5 shows the pin
holding while the two sections below trade places. Each callout stays with its target and inside the window. The
words "Pick where this visitor is:" take their own line, with both region buttons and Next on the next one.

The two regions tell the same story with their own cards: Washington State adds the cable-knit cardigan, looks at the
linen camp shirt and clicks "How to care for wool"; Florida adds the packable rain shell, looks at the cable-knit
cardigan and clicks "Dressing for wet weekends".

## The storyboard without the script

## Where this page departs from the letter of a brief, and why

1. **The sample store's memory is 5 minutes, not the 14-day default.** At 14 days five minutes drain nothing. A brand
   publishes its memory (glossary "Memory", FACTS §1), so the store sets a short one, and step 4's text says so with the
   engine's default beside it. Every other constant is the engine's default.
2. **The first click is an add to the bag.** A release needs an entry first (FACTS §1), and one look gives 0.357,
   below both lines. Only an action weighing 2.7 or more passes the entry line in one click; the engine's shipped add
   to cart weighs 3. So step 2's one click is an add, which both trades two sections and enters, and step 4 can
   release on the engine's arithmetic.
3. **kBlend is 1, the compiled default, where M1 used 0.5.** With the add, kBlend 1 shows every move, and the page
   then matches the engineers' interactive page; this settles review finding L3-5.
4. **Florida's invented leans mirror Washington State's** (linen 1, rain layers 0.75, tableware 0.5, knitwear 0), so
   both regions tell the same seven steps and the sold section is never the first interest.
5. **The carousel section is "Recommended picks",** not "Recommended for you": the check refuses "you" in any string a
   script can write, and the live store is built by its script. The still frames use the same title.
6. **`data-shot="tour-step-N"`, not `"shot-tour-step-N"`.** S1's check pairs `data-shot="<name>"` with
   `shot-<name>.png`, as V3 §5 does; the markdown keeps `shot-tour-step-N.png`.
7. **(Superseded by §8.1.)** The tour carries no spine at all; the four fixed sentences sit verbatim in steps 2, 3, 6
   and 7.
8. **What M1's page held beyond its scenes is not carried** (the gate, "The stylist has more moves", the ten reasons,
   the link cards). §8.2 lists the player and its seven steps; step 7 links What it is and How it works, interactive.
9. **The dial is drawn at zero, not turned.** Turning it is the brand's act, and a lift from this one visit would be an
   invented lift figure. The holdout's share is drawn as one in twenty, the compiled default's 5 percent, said to be the
   brand's setting.
10. **The diversity cap does not appear:** no slot of the sample store has one, and no step calls for it.
11. **Only the step's target is a button in the store;** the other cards are pictures, so the tour cannot drift from
    its seven steps.
12. **The sample store's own color tokens stay in `see-it-work.css`,** scoped to the tour's article, each with its dark
    value (the lead's ruling on M4-8).
13. **Step 4's target is the "Let five minutes pass" button in the store's head,** not a card: time passing is not a
    click on a product, so the control sits where a store's clock would, keeps to the head's right edge and opens its
    callout inward.
14. **The pin is in place when step 5 opens,** not placed by a click. §8.2 gives each step one action, and step 5's
    point is the pin holding while the sections below reorder, so the brand's pin is placed on entering (the section
    marked "pinned", the receipt's newest line naming it) and the action is the story click that reorders the two
    sections below.
15. **Step 7 shows the holdout's page in its panel, with no toggle:** its bar holds only Start again, so the store keeps
    this visitor's page and the panel lists the holdout's page under the dial.
16. **Next before the action holds 1.2 seconds after the 0.7-second glide lands** (1.9 seconds in all), so the result is
    seen settled rather than mid-glide.
17. **The receipt groups an event's lines:** newest event first, and within an event its lines in their own order, so
    at step 4 the release at 4:03 reads above "Five minutes with no clicks; the page is decided again." at 6:00, and a
    cause never reads below its effect. Both are the step's new lines, lit, at the top.
18. **On a phone the receipt's subtitle ("The home page at 6:30, newest line first.") is hidden,** so a changed
    section, its bucket and the newest receipt line share one 390×844 screen; the panel's title and each line's time
    stay.
19. **Without the script the intro line is hidden:** it names Show me and the highlighted target, which only the script
    draws, so the storyboard follows the title directly.
