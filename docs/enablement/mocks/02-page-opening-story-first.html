Revised design mock of the marketing explainer page, written for an internal reader. It opens with the story in one headline and three beats about the engine and the brand, then three contrast cards on how the engine differs from a recommendations engine, from rule-based personalization and from AI that decides, then the animated page that rearranges after one click, then a five-frame walk-through, then the remaining sections in order.

1 · The story, before anything moves

Behavioral Targeting and Intelligence · for marketing

We built an engine that follows each visitor's interest in real time and decides the page at that point in time.

Most visitors to a brand's site arrive as strangers, and most buying happens before any profile exists. Tools built for known customers are blind in that window.

The engine watches what a visitor does on this visit and arranges the page around it, click by click, from the first page on.

The brand's team publishes the rules, keeps the last word, and can read the reason behind every decision.

2 · How it differs from recommendations and from personalization, and why

A recommendations engine

Ranks the past: what people like this visitor bought before.

The engine

Decides the page at the point in time from what this visitor is doing now. The two work side by side; the brand's carousel is one of the modules it orders.

Rule-based personalization

Sorts visitors into segments written in advance. The rules go stale, multiply and fight.

The engine

Needs no segment and no expiration rule. Interest fills with each action and drains on its own, like a bucket.

AI that decides

Chooses in the moment and cannot show its work.

The engine

Calls no AI model to decide. Every decision is arithmetic a person can recompute, on a receipt. Opal is where people ask and generative AI creates.
