# S1: inventory of the repository's explanatory and enablement artefacts

**Research lane S1 · 2 Oct 2026 · internal.** Written for the owner before the enablement package for marketing, account executives and semi-technical sales engineers is built. A read-only survey of the repository as it stood at about noon EDT; this file is the lane's only write. The inventory comes first (tables A to M); then the overlaps and contradictions (§2), what reads best (§3), the gaps (§4), and how it was checked (§5).

**Coverage.** 372 files outside `node_modules/`, `.claude/worktrees/` and `docs/remediation/`: 317 md, 28 html, 15 docx (two are Word lock files), 12 txt. 276 are named by path and 96 sit inside grouped rows, in the 195 rows below. Two buckets are counted separately: 455 Markdown files under `docs/remediation/` (plus 1,897 evidence files that are not documents) and 36 Markdown or HTML duplicates in a stale worktree copy. Eleven of the 372 appeared while the survey ran (table M).

**Legend.** *Generation:* **G1** is the reflex and the shared demo pipeline (Coach, Bright Hour, Meridian, the bank demo); **G2** is the content decision service (tenants, publication sets, receipts, the learn document); "G1 to G2" means written on G1 and patched toward G2; "2025" is the pre-reflex scaffold. *Currency* is judged against the code and `wrangler.toml` on 2 Oct: Current, Mostly current, Partly stale, Stale, Historical (superseded, or point-in-time by its own words). Dates in parentheses are git commit dates unless stated; **18 Sep is a bulk checkpoint commit (`7b01c14`) that touched many files by a few lines and is not an edit date.** "Owner's table" is `docs/Tapestry-BTIE-Working-Notes.md` §1.1. *Hosted:* **shared** is `https://edge-platform.expedge.workers.dev` (runs commit `26e2db1` of 29 Aug); **Top Offers** is `https://edge-platform-top-offers.expedge.workers.dev`; **Vantage** is `https://edge-platform-vantage.expedge.workers.dev`; **staging** and **production** are the customer stamps. *Fit:* Mkt is a marketing reader; SE is a semi-technical sales engineer. In quotations, an em dash is shown as a comma.

**Tables.** A front doors and maps · B the 2025 scaffold and the bank demo · C the BTI customer documents · D field briefs, answer packs, FAQ-style material · E positioning, pricing, delivery motion · F demo documents (F1 Coach, F2 Bright Hour, F3 Opticon and Meridian, F4 Top Offers, F5 Vantage, F6 the kit, F7 LG and OH) · G engine design and architecture · H hand-over and remediation · I HTML pages · J Word documents · K pursuit research · L adjacent and third-party · M created during the survey.

What the best of them share, as design rules:

1. **One sentence first, then an analogy**, never a diagram first. The three best openers are all one sentence.
2. **Tables for comparison and for division of labour** ("them / us", "we do / you do", "before / during / after"), not paragraphs.
3. **Keep the read-alone piece under about 2,200 words** and push the detail behind a link that returns; the Vantage walk-then-detail structure and the one-pager do this.
4. **One figure, one interaction, kept small**: four personas, or a single five-box chain, or sliders each labelled with where the number is published. The 44-beat and 15-beat demo bars teach well in a room but are not readable alone.
5. **End with an honesty ledger or "what not to say."** At least five documents have one (the two newest presenter scripts, BOOTSTRAP §7, the GTM playbook §8, the Opticon run of show); marketing copy would inherit the discipline.
6. **No customer or competitor name in the body**, no work codes (W, D, CW), no "INTERNAL ONLY" banner inside reusable text, no file-and-line citations in prose, no operating commands.
7. **Define a symbol the first time it appears and keep Greek letters off the first screen.** The one-page "why dimensions, not clicks" note is the exception that proves it: it is the right length and the right question and is dense with formulas.
