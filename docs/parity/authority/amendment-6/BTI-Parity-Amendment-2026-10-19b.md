# BTI parity amendment 6 — the learned lift is served on the edge and every miss is told (2026-10-19)

Opened by the remediation lead under the owner's standing rule of 2026-10-16 (R-P78) on the live-acceptance findings of lanes L1, L3 and L7 (P15 M1/M2, P29 R2, P10 F2, P07 C4, P30 X1, P36 CH5, P42 PW3) and the analysis `_evidence/baseline-52/lift-budget/ANALYSIS.md` (track-analyst at 452d2c38; R-P110). Three tickets, P61–P63, in strict order; an independent programme beside amendment 5.

## 1. The finding

On the deployed acceptance pair a published, confirmed learned lift is often not served: 0 of 8 walk decisions and all home-hero readers in L1 and L3, 2 of 6 to 4 of 6 in L7. Two product mechanisms, neither reported anywhere: (A) the lift witness confirms the copy with the statistics object within a fixed 50 ms (`src/learn/fan.ts:43`, unchanged since 7b01c143, sized for an in-process object) on every decision, against a Durable Object hop plus the object's whole-state read, validation and a full-state digest the reader never uses (`src/durable-objects/LearnStats.ts:1583-1603`); the ring read is the same shape at 60 ms (`fan.ts:859`); a miss withholds the lift or the fatigue term silently (`explain.lift: null`, `versions.lift: 0`, nothing in `sources`, no log line, no counter). (B) the witness requires the copy's version to equal the object's latest publication (`fan.ts:41`) although the copy is read from eventually consistent KV and cached 60 s (`src/content/service.ts:108,171`) while the object republishes 30 s after any evidence and even without new evidence (`LearnStats.ts:24,594-602,766,801-806`; `src/learn/stats.ts:747`), so served decisions keep superseding every copy.

## 2. Requirements

**P61 — every lift and ring outcome is told.** On both shopper hosts, every decision's answer names the lift read's outcome per learning slot in `sources.lift` (`served | none | incompatible | late | superseded | unrecognized | unhealthy | failed`, with the milliseconds) and the ring read's outcome in `sources.ring` (`read | late | failed | skipped`, with the milliseconds); the monitor counts each outcome per tenant; the ledger record, the replay equality and the shopper's `decisions[]` do not change (P07 C4, P42 PW3, P49, P50 units unchanged); the witness returns its reason internally while its boolean shape stays for today's callers.

**P62 — the witness tolerates the edge.** (a) The lift witness budget and the ring read budget are exported named constants, overridable by a validated, bounded environment value, with code defaults sized for the edge from the engine's own measured object cost and a per-call round trip, derived in the specification; W38 B7's "a health answer 200 ms late serves nothing" keeps its property as "budget plus margin" in R10 form. (b) The statistics object answers a light witness door from a verdict cached at commit or publish, never re-reading or digesting its whole state; the existing health door stays for operators. (c) A copy whose `{version, digest}` is among the object's recent publications within a bounded window, with the same witness (same configuration, fence and projection) and a healthy state, is served; the record then carries `explain.lift.asOf { version, publishedAt, latest }` only when the copy is not the latest, so no current unit changes. Unchanged: `compatible()`, the refusal of a digest the object never published, the generation and fence binding, `state === 'healthy'`, the P42 `stale` and P49 `republished` members, no lift read on the default arm, the page-scoped witness, the ring's "never extends a decision by more than its budget", and the publish order (`learnPublished` before the KV put).

**P63 — no republication of an unchanged state.** The statistics object's alarm does not publish a new version when nothing changed since the last publication, except when a `stale` or `republished` marker is in force (P42 PW2's rule that the next alarm publication succeeds and clears the marker stays byte for byte).
