# BTI parity amendment 10 — the witness and ring budgets sized for the edge by default (2026-10-22)

Opened by the remediation lead on the owner's decision of 2026-10-22 (R-P236: "raise the limit to 600 instead of 500; we know 600 passes, give ourselves a buffer"), on lanes L12–L15's live findings (`_evidence/parity-step3/live/lanes/L12/REPORT.md` §P70, `CONSOLIDATION-L12/REPORT.md` §P70, `L13/REPORT.md`, `L14/REPORT.md`, `L15/REPORT.md`) and the rulings R-P217(3), R-P219, R-P229 and R-P235 in `_evidence/parity-step3/AMENDMENTS.md`.

## 1. The finding

**The default budgets cut off warm reads.** Amendment 6's P62 set the lift witness budget and the ring read budget to 200 ms each (`src/learn/fan.ts:19`, `:26`), with a bounded per-deployment override (`LIFT_WITNESS_BUDGET_MS`, `RING_READ_BUDGET_MS`, below 5000 ms, refused above it). On the hosted acceptance pair a warm lift read takes 204–378 ms often enough that, at the default, 26 of 706 reads inside a warm tail (3.7 %) and 5 of 24 probe readers on the object host answered `late` and withheld the lift (sixth release, lane L12). With the override at 600 ms and P70's keep-warm (fix round 1), every reader inside a warm tail was served on both hosts (lane L14: 46 of 46; lane L15: every measured lift reader of P29, P30 and P42, six of eight above 200 ms). The lift rows' live acceptance therefore rests on the override (R-P229, R-P230(2)); the Tapestry staging tenant runs the default. The ring read showed the same shape (P30 X5's four LOCK snapshots read the ring `late` at 600 ms; cold ring reads are the ring item's, R-P205(1)).

## 2. The ticket

**P72 — the witness and ring budgets are 600 ms by default.** `LIFT_WITNESS_BUDGET_MS` and `RING_READ_BUDGET_MS` default to 600 ms (`src/learn/fan.ts`), so that a decision with no override admits `{"lift":600,"ring":600}` on both hosts; the bounded override of P62 is unchanged in form (a value below 5000 ms is admitted and reported, a value at or above it is refused and reported in `sources.budgets.refused`, the default then in force); the decision's `sources.budgets` reports the admitted values as today; P62's locks that pin the number 200 are read as the exported defaults (the R10 of R-P195's form, test-only); the kit sentences that state the budgets (kit 02 :369 and the heartbeat/cost paragraph, kit 03's budget and `sources.budgets` statements, the commented `wrangler.toml` lines) state 600 and why (one edge round trip to a warm statistics object on the platform measured at up to 378 ms; the budget bounds the wait, not the read). Nothing else changes: no path, no door, no record shape.

Told to the owner with the ticket: a 600 ms budget means a decision whose lift read is slow waits up to 600 ms before answering without the lift (where today it waits 200 ms); on a warm object the read answers well inside it (largest 478 ms in lane L14), on a cold one (the first decision after more than ten minutes idle or after a deploy) the wait is paid once. The ring read's cold case keeps its own item.
