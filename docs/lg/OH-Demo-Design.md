# OH: the home demo for LG, on the demo kit

**Governing document for the OH demo.** Opened 2026-10-02 by the lead on Simone's standing approval: she asked for a name recommendation and deferred; the recommendation was OH, read as "Optimized Home", and it is taken. The understanding and the ten use cases this demo shows are in [LG-Understanding-and-Use-Cases.md](LG-Understanding-and-Use-Cases.md); the chassis is the demo kit, [Demo-Kit-Design.md](../demo-kit/Demo-Kit-Design.md), proven on Vantage. Decisions are logged at the end; a lane that needs a decision not logged here stops and asks the lead, not the owner.

---

## 1. What OH is

A fictional home brand in LG's palette, selling LG's real products by their real names and images, on the content decision service. Three categories in the first build: **TVs with soundbars**, **laundry**, and **one kitchen path** (range → over-the-range microwave → dishwasher → kitchen package), about 500 products from the crawled taxonomy (`research/lg-taxonomy.json`). Monitors, laptops and the rest of the kitchen come later.

**Name and look.** OH. Wordmark "OH." in LG red (#A50034) on white; charcoal (#1D1D1B) text; grey (#6B6B6B) secondary; a light panel (#F5F5F5); the kit's director keeps its own dark palette. Inter for the page, JetBrains Mono for the machine panels. No LG mark, no "Life's Good".

**Worker and tenant.** Worker `edge-platform-oh`, queue `events-oh`, dev port 9120, tenant `oh`, session host, demo profile, its own D1 rows and durable objects, generated from `wrangler.toml` by `build-demo-worker-config.mjs`. Creating the worker, its queues, its tenant and its secrets is a one-time owner approval; everything before that is local.

**Honesty.** The visitor's region is always real, read from the request; the regional lean is synthetic and labelled "synthetic → warehouse" until LG's rows replace it; census is shown and never scored; price, financing and rebate eligibility never vary by geography or inferred income; nothing sits near a financing line; no recognition across devices without sign-in; attach is measured, never claimed; a retailer referral is not a sale. The lines of §6 of the understanding document apply to every piece of copy.

## 3. Dimensions

Sixteen, within the registry's 32: `category`, `subcategory`, `type` (French door, front-load, OLED, QNED), `series`, `panel`, `sizeBand`, `capacityTier`, `fuel`, `finish`, `priceTier` (category-relative: good, better, best, flagship), `room`, `feature`, `intent` (replace, remodel, new-home, upgrade; asserted or inferred), `contentType` (deal, compare, explainer, tool), `retailer`, `package`.

Decay for the walk, as Vantage compresses it: `contentType` and `retailer` 60 seconds; `priceTier`, `intent`, `feature` about 10 minutes; `type`, `series`, `sizeBand`, `capacityTier`, `fuel`, `finish`, `room` long (the published τ names 60 days; the demo's "three weeks later" beat is a labelled clock jump, as Vantage's exit beat is a timed re-ask). The regional allowlist (`learn.regional.dimensions`) is `category`, `type`, `sizeBand`, `fuel`; `priceTier` is out of the regional lean until LG's own rows exist (R4 ruling, 2026-10-02), and the synthetic lean carries no census term. Products stay out of the content catalog; their attributes travel on events and on `/sort`, as Vantage's phones do.

## 9. The store: a working site first, the scripted walk second

**Ruling, 2026-10-02 (Simone, on opening the hosted OH):** a demo is a working site. Every visible control does what a visitor expects, the engine responds to her own clicks exactly as to the director's presses, and the site looks like the customer's site, not like the previous demo's frame. "Off limits" for the buy box means the engine never decides it, never that it is dead. The scaffold's empty slots and Vantage's chrome are the kit's gap; the fix is here and in the kit.

### 9.1 The look: LG's, not Vantage's

Tokens in `oh.css`, overriding the kit's defaults: page `#FFFFFF`; ink `#1D1D1B`; secondary `#6B6B6B`; panels `#F5F5F5`; lines `#E6E6E6`; brand `#A50034` (the wordmark, primary buttons, "$X OFF"); brand deep `#8A0029`; no yellow, no black bars. Inter for the page. A white header, 64 px: wordmark "OH.", the category nav (TVs, Laundry, Kitchen, Offers, Support), a search box, "Sign in", a cart icon with its count, the ZIP chip with the real region. Product cards on white: image, name, stars and review count, sale price bold, "$X OFF" in brand, the struck list price in secondary, the estimated delivery line, a primary button. Buttons: brand fill, white text, 4 px radius; secondary buttons outlined in ink. Section headings 24 px, ink. The service strip on the panel colour. Density and whitespace as lg.com's grids (four cards per row at 1440). The director (the kit's machine panel) is a presenter's tool: docked right, collapsible to a slim rail, collapsed by default, expanded by the presenter's key or `?present=1`, its dark palette unchanged.

### 9.2 The boot page is a real store

Before any visitor exists, `/oh/` renders the published template: the opening catalog's pieces in the slots' template order (pins and defaults), with real content in every module, in lg.com's anatomy: pencil banner; header; the 13-icon category nav; the hero carousel; the service strip (free delivery, 14-day returns, extended coverage, haul-away, chat with an expert); the room tiles; "Discover OH deals" (the deals four); "Shop with an expert"; "Recommended for you" (the product rail); the showcase tabs (appliances, entertainment); the benefits of buying here; the footer. The first visitor is minted on the first click or on the director's first press, not on load, so the boot page is the site as a stranger would see it and Compare's "the site's own page" is a page.

### 9.3 Every control works by hand
