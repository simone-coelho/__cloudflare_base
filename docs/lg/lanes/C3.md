# Lane C3 — the on-screen copy is gender neutral

Copy lane, briefed by the lead on 2026-10-03 after W1c's revision of the presenter script reported that the
store's and the kit's on-screen copy is gendered ("Nothing in her profile moved", "Completes what she committed
to", beat labels and notes that say "her" and "she"). Simone's standing rule: everything written for her or on
her behalf is gender neutral; a visitor, shopper, customer or reader is "the visitor", "the shopper" or "they",
never "she" or "he". The demo's pages are written on her behalf, so the rule applies to every string a room can
read: beat labels, notes and receipts, the director's panel, the Just now strip, the before/now strips, toasts,
sheets, the sign-in flow, the retailer modal, the cart drawer, the phone, the report.

## What the lane does

1. **Find every gendered string a room can read.** `public/oh/beats.json`, `public/oh/beats/*.json` (only if
   a string there is shown; the seed rewrites these files, so change their *source* in
   `scripts/demo-kit/configs/oh/*.json` or wherever the seed reads the copy, never the generated copies alone;
   say in the report where each string's source is), `public/oh/oh-store.js`, `public/oh/oh.js` (the shared, P2
   and P3 sections now; the P1 section after lane M1d hands back, as a second pass), `public/oh/index.html`,
   `public/oh/product.html`, `public/oh/m.html`, and the kit: `public/demo-kit/panels.js`,
   `public/demo-kit/deltas.js`, `public/demo-kit/prove.js`, `public/demo-kit/sheets.js`,
   `public/demo-kit/compare.js`, `public/demo-kit/store.js`, `public/demo-kit/transport.js` (notes it writes),
   and `public/demo-kit/director.js` after M1d hands back. Search for ` her `, `her.`, `her,`, `hers`, `she`,
   `She`, `herself`, `his`, `him`, `he `, `He ` in strings, and read each hit in context (a "her" inside a
   product's own name or a quoted customer review is not ours to change; nothing of that kind is expected).
   Code comments are not on screen; change them too where cheap, never at the cost of a diff that hides the
   on-screen changes.
2. **Rewrite each string** so it reads naturally: "the visitor", "the shopper", "they", "their", or recast the
   sentence ("Nothing in the profile moved", "Completes what was committed to", "the visitor's three strongest
   signals"). Keep every number, every name of a dimension, every receipt's structure and every length budget
   (the panel's rows and the strips are measured by the suites). Where a beat's note is quoted by the presenter
   script (`docs/oh/OH-Presenter-Script.md`, lane W1c), tell the lead the old and new wording so the script's
   quote can follow; do not edit the script.
3. **The named fictional account** in beat 15 and the sign-in ("Dana Whitfield") keeps the name; the pronoun
   for Dana is "they" as well, or the sentence is recast around the name.
4. **The kit's strings serve Vantage too.** Every change in `public/demo-kit/*` must keep Vantage's two suites at
   their counts (`scripts/rehearse-vantage-page.mjs` 115 and `scripts/rehearse-vantage-dom.mjs` 298; read
   `docs/verizon/RESUME-vantage.md` for how they run) and OH's suites green on the strings they assert (the DOM
   suite reads the Just now strip, the panel's rows, the sign-in sheet, the before/now strips: run
   `node scripts/rehearse-oh-dom.mjs --base http://localhost:9150` and `node scripts/rehearse-oh-page.mjs --base
   http://localhost:9150`; where a suite asserts an old gendered string, the suite is lane S2's: name the line
   and the new string in your report and tell S2 through the lead, never edit the suites yourself).
5. **Two passes.** The first pass now, on everything but the P1 section of `oh.js` and `director.js`, which
   lane M1d is editing; hand back a consistent state and a report. The second pass when the lead says M1d has
   handed back: those two files, the same way.

  `public/oh/beats/`; if you must seed, use `--out` under your scratchpad).
