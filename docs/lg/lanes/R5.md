# Lane R5: the visual review, lg.com beside OH

**Opened 2026-10-02 by the lead; starts when M1b's restyle and A1's regenerated art are in the working tree.** The reviewer edits nothing but the Report section of this file and the screenshots it writes under `docs/lg/lanes/shots/R5-*.png`. The question is the one Simone asked: does OH look like lg.com? The answer is a side-by-side, never a description.

## Method

1. In a fresh Chrome profile at 1440 wide, and again at 390, take lg.com (left) and OH (right) for: the homepage (top, and scrolled to the deals and the product cards), the TVs page (lg.com `/us/tvs` beside OH `/oh/?view=tvs`), and a product page (lg.com's OLED65C6PUA beside OH's `/oh/product?id=OLED65C6PUA`; a washer pair likewise). OH is a local rig of your own (port 9142, `.wrangler/state-r5`, provisioned with `scripts/demo-kit/rig.mjs --config oh` and seeded with `seed.mjs --config oh`; snapshot `public/oh/beats/*.json` by hash and use `--out` for later seeds; restore at the end) opened cold with no director and no visitor, then once more after a manual session of three clicks so the decided state shows.
2. Judge region by region against section 10.1 of [OH-Demo-Design.md](../OH-Demo-Design.md): the top bar; the header (height, the nav words, the icons); the hero (art slides, peeking neighbours, headline size and weight and colour treatment, the button, the badges, arrows and dots); the service strip; the category discs; the deals bento (image tiles with headlines over them, tabs); the expert banner; the product cards (image, name, stars, price bold, "$X OFF" red, struck list price, delivery line, button); type scale and weights; section rhythm; the grid's filter rail; the product page's buy bar, gallery and tabs proportions. For each region: MATCH, CLOSE (what differs, in one line), or OFF (what is wrong, in one line, and what lg.com does instead).
3. The verdict is PASS only when a room would read OH as a site in lg.com's family at a glance at 1440 and at 390, with no region OFF and the hero, the cards and the header at least CLOSE. Otherwise FAIL with the regions to change listed first, each with the lg.com reference screenshot region to match.
4. Confirm nothing else regressed: the DOM suite's boot and manual-session checks still pass on your rig (report the counts); every decided slot is in the engine's order; the director opens with D and closes; no overflow and no broken image at 1440, 1366 and 390.

### What to change first, to go from CLOSE to MATCH (each with its lg.com reference)

1. **The action colour and shape, on every button and every "$X OFF".** lg.com's is `#EA1917` with a 32 px pill radius. OH's is `#A50034` (the colour of LG's *logo*) with a 4 px radius. A room reads OH's buttons as burgundy, not lg.com's red. This is the most noticeable "not quite" at a glance on both widths. Reference: every button in `R5-1440-home-expert-cards` and `R5-1440-product-tv`. **This needs an owner ruling:** §9.1 and §10.1 specify the brand colour at 4 px, and S2's suite asserts it. §10.1's "buttons red … 4 px radius" is itself not what lg.com's CSS does.
2. **The hero.** A full-bleed 540 px slide; the headline at weight 500 to 600, not 800; lg.com's progress bars and pause on the art. Reference: `R5-1440-home-top`. Lighter banner and bento headlines go with it.
3. **The header at 1440.** 80 px tall, 18 px nav, and icons (search, account) in place of the search field and the "Delivering to … / Sign in" text. Reference: `R5-1440-home-top`. Also the top bar in charcoal with white text, which is one line of CSS.
4. **The phone at 390.** A header that scrolls away. The deals and Recommended rails as one-at-a-time carousels with pagers. The tab row on one scrolling line. The product page's buy bar docked to the bottom. References: `R5-390-home-top`, `R5-390-home-deals`, `R5-390-home-cards`, `R5-390-product-tv`.
5. **The category row.** Larger tiles (six per view at 1440) with a pager instead of a clipped ninth tile. Reference: `R5-1440-home-top` second screen.
