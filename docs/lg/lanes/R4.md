  - **Why the gas dryer leads.** DLGX6501W ($1,299, gas) is first in `/sort` because of `retailer:lg-com` 0.654, `retailer:best-buy-pickup` 0.497 and `package:laundry-4-piece` 0.504: it is sold here and is in a set.
