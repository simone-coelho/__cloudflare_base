## 3. Derivation rules (products.json)

- **Scope.** Rows come in three ways: a listing node's `listedOn` membership (TVs, soundbars, laundry, ranges, over-the-range microwaves, dishwashers, kitchen packages, laundry sets, and wall mounts from the TV accessories listing); the crawl's variant-chip rows matched by SKU pattern; and the named-only parts in note ³. Refrigerators are included only as package members. Projector remotes, stands, filters, cooktops and wall ovens are left out (LTIS7338XE stays in because LG lists it among the induction ranges).
- **Category, subcategory, type.** `category` is LG's product-kind level (its `categoryCodes` level 2–3, e.g. `tvs`, `washers`, `ranges`), so that price quartiles compare like with like. `subcategory` is LG's listing node (`oled-evo-tvs`, `front-load-washers`, `gas-ranges`). `type` is the configuration a shopper picks: `oled`, `qned`, `micro-rgb`, `uhd`, `hd`, `lifestyle`; `front-load`, `top-load`, `pedestal-washer`; `front-load` or `rear-control` dryers; `slide-in` or `freestanding`; `top-control` or `front-control`; `3-piece` or `4-piece`. Sources, in order: listing, URL slug, crawl name, SKU letters.
- **Series** comes from the name or SKU, never from a filter, since lg.com has no series filter. TVs: the SKU token (`c6`, `g6`, `b6`, `qned85`, `qned8m`, `mrgb95`, `nu85`, `ua77`…). Laundry: LG's model family (also kept per row as `modelFamily`, e.g. `4000`), `4000-series` and so on, shared by a washer and its dryers. Kitchen: the line, `lg`, `lg-studio` or `lg-signature`. Soundbars: the model.
- **Panel (TVs).** `oled-evo` for OLED C, G, M and W (or an evo listing or name); `oled` for B and T; `mini-led` when the name says Mini LED, the model year is 2026 (every named 2026 QNED says Mini LED) or it is listed as QNED evo; otherwise `qned`; `micro-rgb`; `nano` for NU; `led` for the rest.
- **sizeBand.** TVs take the inch digits in the SKU, banded by LG's own Screen Size filter: `up-to-41`, `42-54`, `55-64`, `65-74`, `75-82`, `83-and-up`. Appliances carry a band only where the crawl states a width: `24-inch` for dishwashers named "24 Inch", `30-inch` for LSGL6335X.
- **capacityTier.** Cu. ft. comes from the crawl name, a laundry-set name (which states both machines), a product-page spec, or a colour variant's name. For ranges and over-the-range microwaves it comes from the SKU's capacity digits, a rule that all 22 named ranges and all 10 named microwaves in the crawl obey. The tiers (`compact`, `standard`, `large`, `extra-large`) follow LG's Capacity filter bands for washers and fixed cuts elsewhere (`capacityTierRule`).
- **fuel.** Ranges: listing membership (induction, then dual fuel, gas, electric), then URL, then the SKU's third letter. Dryers: URL, listing or `DLG`, where a heat pump (`DLHC`) counts as electric. WashTowers and sets: from the SKU or the name.
- **finish.** The laundry colour letter: W white, B black steel, V graphite steel and A alpine white are all evidenced by the crawl's variant labels and set names. P platinum black is inferred: it is the one washer colour on LG's filter that no other letter explains. Kitchen finishes come from package names ("in Stainless Steel", "in Black Stainless Steel"), variant labels and the evidenced SKU letters: S and F/Y/T stainless, X PrintProof stainless, D black stainless, Z PrintProof black stainless. Unknown letters (M, L, K, the SIGNATURE S) are left empty.
- **priceTier** is category-relative: quartiles of the crawl's sale prices within the category (`priceCuts`), with ≤ q1 `good`, ≤ median `better`, ≤ q3 `best`, above that `flagship`. No price means no tier.
- **room**: `living-room`, `laundry-room` or `kitchen`. **feature** (up to 8): name keywords, plus the C6 sample page's spec applied series-wide (165Hz, G-Sync, Dolby Vision and Atmos, Filmmaker Mode; Brightness Booster except on the 42-inch). `bright-room` goes on G-series and Mini LED TVs, following the research's signal table, which marks it as inference. **package**: the package kinds a row belongs to. **retailer**: `lg-com` (cart), `best-buy-pickup` (the card's Est. In-Store Pickup or the sample page's bopis flag) and `find-a-retailer`.
- **Prices** are the crawl's, never computed. Rows with no price are null; there are 59. The crawl copied one Sound Suite card's price ($2,999.94) onto every Sound Suite row, so seven rows take their own card's text instead, and M7-P2_W7 is null. Package `memberSum` and `savings` appear only when every member's price is in the crawl.
- **cta** is `cart` where the card showed Add to Cart, or Notify Me (with `inStock` false). It is `retailer` where the card showed Find a Retailer, or where no card was seen; there, `ctaSource` says the retailer is assumed, because we never claim the cart without evidence. Packages and sets are `cart`, since they are sold only online (lg-site-recon.md §3).
- **Names.** 247 come from the crawl. 355 are derived for rows the crawl saw only as cards, following LG's own naming: "77 Inch Class LG OLED evo AI G6 4K Smart TV 2026", "4.5 cu. ft. Front Load Washer (WM4000HWA)", "6.3 cu. ft. Gas Slide-in Range (LSGL6335X)". Each is marked in `nameSource`. **URLs.** 87 are LG's slug pattern applied to a sibling and marked `urlSource` (not visited); two rows have none.
- **fits** (inches, 32 rows), only where the crawl has them: the six sample product pages and their colour variants (OLED65C6PUA, WM6500HWA/HBA, DLEX4000B/W, LSGL6335X, LDNTH753S/D, S70TY), the pedestal and SideKick sizes in the washer and dryer specs, "24 Inch" dishwasher names (nominal) and the "29''" stacking kit.
- **family** is the product family a product page asserts as `ctx.family`, beside `ctx.anchor` = the row's own `sku` (E1's ruling, 2026-10-02: the engine's complements rule reads `ctx.anchor` as a product id). There are 28 values, listed in `catalog.json` under `contextSignals.family`. The field was called `anchor` until that ruling. The laundry model number family, which was the row's `family` before, is now `modelFamily`, so that the two never collide.
- **Companions** follow our merchandiser's rules, stated in `companionRules` and ordered in stock first, sold here first, same finish first:
  - washer → dryers of the same model family, split into electric, gas and heat pump. The family numbering (gas NNNN+1, heat pump NNNN+2) is LG's own, as the WM6500HWA and DLEX4000B pages and the laundry sets pair them.
  - front-load only → the WDP6 drawer and WD300 SideKick in the washer's colour, then any pedestal in that colour, and KSTK4. Top-loaders get none, with a `pedestalsNote` that says why (beat 6).
  - TV → soundbars of the TV series' tier: G, W, M, T and Micro RGB flagship; C and QNED 9 best; B, QNED 8 and lifestyle better; the rest good. QNED leads with the S70TY, which its page marks "Designed for QNED TV".
  - TV → mounts by VESA pattern. Only the OLED65C6PUA (300 x 200 → OLW480B) has one in the crawl.
  - range ↔ microwave ↔ dishwasher → first what LG packages together, then the same finish family and line.
  - package → members.

## 4. The catalog (catalog.json)

- **Slots and types.** `hero` (hero; 5), `continue` (hero; 1, stage-gated by `journeyStageFit`), `category-rail` (tile; 7), `deals` (promotion; 14 including the launch pin), `grid-promo` (7: four deals reused, plus the size, series-chart and Best Buy pickup tools as `module`), `pdp-modules` (35: 23 modules and 12 `package` pieces), `pdp-offers` (promotion; 12). No slot outside design §2 is used. If a slots document ever sets `allowedTypes`, it must admit `package` in `pdp-modules` and `module` in `grid-promo`.
- **Gates.** Every product-page piece (47: the 35 in `pdp-modules` and the 12 in `pdp-offers`) carries `eligibleWhen.context.family` with the families it serves. With no family asserted, none of them is eligible: the home page and the grid see none, because the gate fails closed. A sku sent as `ctx.anchor` without `ctx.family` opens no gate either. Checked with the engine's own matcher:
  - the pedestal and stacking kit serve front-load washers and dryers only (beat 6);
  - a gas range sees only gas packages;
  - Best Buy pickup is offered on TVs, soundbars and laundry, never on ranges, dishwashers, refrigerators or packages, which is lg.com's own rule.
- **Complements** (design §6 item 1, as E1 landed it). 22 pieces list the skus they complete; a slot whose `complements` rule is set prefers them when the page names one of those skus as `ctx.anchor`, and the receipt shows a completes line. The family gate stays the fail-closed guard. Each module's list is exactly the rows inside its gate whose own relation for it is non-empty, so a preferred card is never empty:
  - `mod_tv_soundbar` 146 TVs; `mod_tv_mount` 1 (the OLED65C6PUA, the only TV with a VESA pattern in the crawl);
  - `mod_laundry_dryer` 41 washers; `mod_laundry_washer` 85 dryers; `mod_laundry_pedestal` 82 and `mod_laundry_stacking` 75 front-load washers and dryers; `mod_laundry_pair_price` 30 front-load machines that belong to a laundry set;
  - `mod_range_microwave` 76 ranges; `mod_range_dishwasher` 111 ranges and microwaves; `mod_kitchen_range` 74 microwaves and dishwashers;
  - each of the 12 package pieces lists itself and the members its gate shows: range, microwave and dishwasher for kitchen (the refrigerator is not a product page here), washer and dryer for laundry. A package therefore completes the range it contains, never another range of the same fuel.

  On WM4000HWA the dryer, pedestal and stacking modules complete it; on the top-loader WT8400CW only the dryer module does. On OLED65C6PUA the soundbar and mount modules both complete it; the 55-inch C6 gets the soundbar alone.
  
  The slots document (K4 or P2) must set `complements: { weight }` on `pdp-modules` for the preference to apply. Without it the pieces rank as before, on the family gate and her taste.
- **Windows** are the crawl's CMS instants:
  - the gift card, the matching-soundbar and Sound Suite offers, and free wall mounting end 2026-10-05T03:59:59Z (10/4 Eastern). The free-mounting start was not captured, so it opens on the crawl day;
  - the laundry pair and set offers end 11/3;
  - range plus microwave, packages and refrigerator plus dishwasher end 10/21;
  - the ThinQ warranty ends 12/31.
  
  The catalog's top-level `anchor` is the windows' instant (`2026-10-01T12:00:00.000Z`) for the beats' anchor file. It is not the context signal. Big screens, the Xbox controller and WASH1000 had no dates in the crawl, so they carry no window.
- **Beat hooks.**
  - **11:** the gift-card trio closes about 3.7 days after the anchor, and `deal_big_screens` takes the place. Two package pieces are published `inStock: false` because a member showed Notify Me on 2026-10-01, which is the defect lg.com shows today: `pkg_select_4_induction` (LSIL6332FE) and `pkg_deluxe_4_gas` (LRMXS3006S). A beat may open with one of them true and publish the crawl's truth so the room sees it leave.
  - **12:** `pin_micro_rgb_evo` in `hero` and `deals`, pinned by the slot.
  - **5–8:** the companion modules, each completing the product on the page by sku.
  - **9:** the OLED and QNED comparisons and the size-up tool.
  - **10:** `mod_fit_check`.
  - **13:** `hero_continue`.
  - **16:** `mod_where_to_buy_pickup` (`outcome: partner_pickup`) and `mod_where_to_buy_retailer` (`outcome: retailer_referral`).
- **Vocabulary.** Once any piece tags a dimension, an event value that no piece names builds no taste (`admittedTouches`, src/reflex/core.ts; the vocabulary comes from catalog tags). The seven rail tiles therefore name every value their products carry, and the validator proves that every product value on every tagged dimension is named.
- **Copy** holds the lines of the understanding document's §6:
  - nothing about financing or monthly amounts, and no price varying by place or person ("the price is the same for everyone" on every TV comparison);
  - "goes with" is always "our merchandiser's rule, shown as one";
  - Find a Retailer says "we count a referral, not a sale";
  - the induction rebate is "state-run and income-qualified: check your own eligibility… we never pre-qualify anyone";
  - no attach or lift claims, no census, no LG mark.
  
  Every dollar amount in the copy is an offer amount the crawl shows, or a product's crawl price, sum or saving.

## 5. The regional vocabulary (region.json)

The section uses the kit's own model (`income-tilt`, scripts/demo-kit/lib/region.mjs), so `region-seed.mjs --config` runs it as it is:

- **Texas and New Jersey are written verbatim** as rehearsal regions A and B. Texas leans QNED, 75–82 inches, electric; it is the asserted contrast for beat 2. New Jersey leans OLED, 65–74 inches, and cooks 70% gas.
- **The other 49 regions blend the TV rows** (`category: tvs`, keyed by type, size band and price tier) between the two by census income.
- **Appliance rows are one national mix**, carried as extras. Cooking fuel is about a third gas in 48 states and DC, and 70% gas in California. With New Jersey that makes about 38% of households, the research's figures. Dryers are 80% electric everywhere. Every region holds 686 to 711 units in 29 to 34 rows, inside the seed route's 64/400/800 limits.

Built through the kit, these are the leaders the panel would read:

| region | category | type | sizeBand | fuel | gas cooking | electric drying |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NC | tvs | oled | 65-74 | electric | 33% | 80% |
| TX | tvs | qned | 75-82 | electric | 33% | 80% |
| NJ | tvs | oled | 65-74 | gas | 70% | 80% |
| CA | tvs | oled | 65-74 | gas | 70% | 80% |
| NY, FL, MS, MA | tvs | oled | 65-74 | electric | 33% | 80% |

So the beat 1 line holds for a visitor in North Carolina: "your sales here lean OLED 65-inch". Labels read "<State>: synthetic home sales, national mix tilted by census income", always synthetic. The vocabulary is exactly the product values on the five allowlisted dimensions.

## 6. Validation (all pass)

The engine's own validator was bundled from `src/content/kinds.ts` and `src/content/lifecycle.ts` by the project's esbuild in WSL, with a negative control: a missing `slotTypes`, a reversed window, a `ctx.`-prefixed signal, a duplicate tag and a `complements` that is not an array of strings are each refused. It was rebundled after E1's change to `kinds.ts`. Against that validator:

- `validateContentCatalog` accepts the publishable document (76 pieces) and returns every piece unchanged;
- every engine field is in `PIECE_FIELDS`, and the document carries only `version` and `pieces`;
- every window is two whole ISO instants (23 windowed pieces);
- all 249 `featuredProductIds`, all 761 `complements` (22 pieces), every `display.imageSku` and every gate value resolve. Every gate is `ctx.family`, none `ctx.anchor`. Every complemented product sits inside the piece's own gate, and package complements are only the package and its members;
- the vocabulary is covered as §4 describes; the pieces tag exactly the sixteen dimensions; multi-valued attributes stay within 8 values;
- all 2,578 companion relations resolve; no top-loader has a pedestal; dryer lists split by the dryer's own fuel and stay inside the family;
- every sale and list price equals a crawl number for that SKU, and package sums recompute;
- the publishable catalog is 63.5 KiB (115.9 KiB with `display`), about a thirtieth of the 2 MiB cap;
- the copy checks of §4 hold, and the gate checks of §4 hold on the engine's matcher.

`scripts/demo-kit/lib/config.mjs` validates the region section. The kit builds 51 regions, all rows pass the seed route's own checks, and the leaders above hold.

## 7. What the crawl lacked

These are build items for whoever next reads the product pages. D2 visits them for imagery; extending that pass is the lead's call.

1. **Widths for beat 10.** Only 2 laundry models have product dimensions (WM6500HWA, DLEX4000B, with their colour variants). The fit check cannot hide "3 wider than your space" until the laundry pages' "Product (WxHxD)" values are read. Dishwasher widths are nominal, from names.
2. **VESA patterns.** Only the OLED65C6PUA's (300 x 200). Other TV pages show the mount module's honest empty state.
3. **Names.** 355 rows were card-only or variant-only and carry derived names. The product pages' titles should replace them.
4. **Images.** 356 rows have none; D2 fills them.
5. **Prices.** 59 rows have none: schema rows priced 0, Sound Suite M7-P2_W7, and the named-only parts. KSTK4, WDP6W/B and WD300CW/B/V have no crawl price, so laundry-set savings cannot be computed and the set pieces show the set price alone. Their white and black counterparts WDP6A ($249) and WD300CA ($659) are priced.
6. **Calls to action.** 247 schema-only rows had no card, so `cta` is assumed `retailer`. Ratings exist only on card rows.
7. **Listing caps.** A listing shows at most 48 products (/us/dryers showed 48 of 111, /us/ranges 48 of 93). The sub-listings recover most of the rest, but not all: 81 of lg.com's 111 dryers were seen on a listing, 52 of its 75 washers and 79 of its 93 ranges. The variant chips and named parts add to these.
8. **Capacity.** 18 of the 51 front- and top-load washers and 21 of the 88 dryers have no capacity in any crawl text. Most are current Add to Cart top-loaders and card-only front-loads. No SideKick pedestal washer has one.
9. **Offer data.** The free-mounting start date is missing, and so are the dates of big screens, the Xbox controller and WASH1000. Which TVs qualify for free mounting is not in the crawl: the C6 rows are used, from the sample page.
10. **Finishes.** Colour letters M, L, K and the SIGNATURE S are unknown, and P is inferred.

## 8. For the lead and the next lanes

- **Product pages (P2, P3).** Send `ctx.family = <row.family>` and `ctx.anchor = <row.sku>` on every product-page decision; the home page and the grid send neither. Read `family` (not `anchor`) from `products.json`, and `modelFamily` for the laundry series.

- **K4's `public/oh/demo.config.json`.** `region.vocabulary` is a provisional list, and its own note says it must equal D1's. It should be set to `region.json`'s `vocabulary` (16 categories, 28 types, 8 size bands, 4 tiers, 4 fuels), and so should E1's or K4's `SEED_VOCABULARY` in the OH config. The authoritative per-dimension vocabulary for the registry and the seed's local checks is `products.json` `dimensions.vocabulary`, plus `intent` and `contentType` from the pieces. The config's `dimensions[].values` are samples.
- **Product categories.** K4's `products.categories` omits five categories the file carries: washtowers, washer-dryer-combos, styler, laundry-accessories and refrigerators.
- **The continue slot.** It is gated in the config by `visit: return`. The piece itself carries `journeyStageFit` [considering, deciding] and no `eligibleWhen`, so a piece-level visit gate would be an addition.
- **The validator and the build scripts.** If they should live in the repository, a lane that owns `scripts/**` (S1 or K4) can take them from this report's checks. They are `build-oh.mjs`, `build-catalog.mjs` and `validate-oh.mjs` in the session scratchpad, with `kinds.bundle.mjs` bundled by `node_modules/.bin/esbuild` in WSL.
