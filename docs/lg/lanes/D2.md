### How the images were taken

- **The taxonomy's links are LG's 350-pixel listing thumbnails.** For those rows the URL fetched is the same asset at LG's own product-page gallery rendition (`ecomm-PDPGallery-1100x730`, a 730 square for LG's square masters) on the same host; the crawl's link is kept as `listed` in the config.
- **Rows without a link:** the product page fetched once, its primary image read from the page's server data (`__NEXT_DATA__`, `productData.product.mediaAssets`), the first gallery image that is the product itself by LG's alt text and file name.
- **LG's first gallery image is usually a marketing composite.** On nearly every appliance and on some TVs it carries LG's "America's Most Reliable" shield, a fuel icon, ENERGY STAR, a CNET "People's Pick" card or a Consumer Reports seal; other gallery entries are infographics, review-quote cards, panel close-ups and room scenes. So each candidate was triaged on the kit's own cut (a scratchpad detector for detached red or blue marks, green seals, detached text, scenes and cards), the first clean one taken, and every result then looked at; 114 choices were made by eye on candidate sheets showing each product's whole gallery.
- **The key.** The kit's default tolerance (36) flood-fills into white appliances on LG's white ground (a white top-loader lost almost all of its body), so each source carries its own `keyTol`: the noise its ground actually shows on the border, 1 on 577 images (LG's ground is pure white), at most 8. White bodies, bezels and frames are whole at 1:1. The cost: a faint light fringe at some edges and a few near-white specks, invisible on white or the light panel, visible on a dark ground.
- Raw downloads are cached outside the checkout (scratchpad `d2/cache`, 1,492 files, 50 MB) and never committed.
