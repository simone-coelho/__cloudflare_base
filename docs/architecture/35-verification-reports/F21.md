**Probe 1 — the audit's hero/rail example.** Ring: `d-hero` (slot hero, item A) and `d-rail` (slot rail,

The slot is on the record and is discarded. **Confirmed.**

**Probe 1b — the necessary condition the audit omits.** A hero click on item A credits a rail only when the
rail served the same item, or a piece featuring the clicked product. A rail serving a different piece is not
credited under `direct`. Under `match:'any'` every slot is credited regardless.

**Probe 1c — the mirror case.** Ring holds only `d-rail` (slot rail, item A); the click names slot hero.
Credits = `[["rail","d-rail"]]`. The credit lands on a placement the shopper's event never named.

**Probe 2 — CW32 widens it.** Three slots (hero, rail, tile) each serving a different piece that features
`SKU-9`. One purchase naming `SKU-9` and `slot:'hero'` produces **three** credits, one per slot. The
featured-product rule multiplies the blast radius of the missing slot constraint.

**Probe 3 — position confound, quantified.** One rail, `take: 5`, five items of *identical* true quality;

## 3 · Verdict

**Confirmed.** Every evidence claim holds at HEAD. The one cited line that is imprecise (`policy.ts:74` names
the function, not the branch) does not affect the conclusion. Both defects in the title are real, independent
and simultaneously live:

1. `attribute()` never compares `outcome.slot` to `e.slot`, so an interaction that names a placement can pay
   a different one, and pays every slot that served the same item or a piece featuring the same product.
2. `n` in the statistics is decisions **served**, not content **seen**, and position is not in the pooling
   key, so rank and visibility are indistinguishable from content quality.

## 4 · Severity and size

**Severity: blocks the pilot. I agree with the audit.** Reasoning, on the brief's own scale:

- It is not *blocks launch*. `gammaOf` defaults to 0 (`src/content/service.ts:220`), so lift is shadow and
  the confounded numbers do not change what is served unless a slot is configured with `gamma > 0`. The
  brief's rule allows a learning feature to stay disabled provided it is **reported as undelivered**.
- It is not *fix before the next customer*. Rails and carousels are the pilot's own surface (`DEFAULT_SLOTS`
  ships `rail take 5`, `story take 2`), and lift, its evidence level and its cell are shown on every receipt
  and in the operator console today. The measurement is wrong for the pilot brand, not only for the next one.
- The consequence reaches an operator even in shadow: `src/learn/autonomy.ts:28` builds "tag value →
  exposure-weighted mean lift over the items carrying it" and `src/learn/cycle.ts:63` turns that into weight-
  change proposals a human approves. Position and cross-slot confounds therefore arrive as recommendations
  with a number attached. The audit's consequence stops one step short of this.

**Size: M. I agree, with a split.** The slot constraint alone is **S** (§7). Position bucketing is genuinely
M: `Level` is typed `0|1|2|3|4|5`, `LEVEL_WORDS` has six entries, `depth()`/`parentKey()` do string
arithmetic on `|`, and `liftFor`, `buildSnapshot`, the receipt, replay and `foldDecisions` all assume that
ladder. Impression-gated exposures are the other M half: a wire change, an SDK change and a new dedupe in
`LearnStats`. Together, M.

## 5 · What the audit got wrong

1. **`src/learn/policy.ts:74` is the function signature**, not the direct-match branch. That is lines 81–85.
   Minor, but a remediator jumping to :74 sees a signature.
2. **The hero/rail claim is stated more broadly than it is true.** "Credited both placements" needs the same
   item — or a shared featured product — in both slots. A hero click on A does not credit a rail showing B
   under `direct`. Under `match:'any'` it does. Worth stating so the team does not over- or under-estimate
   the population of affected credits.
3. **"Use decision ID … for direct interaction credit" is not currently implementable.** The client-side
   `ContentDecision` (`src/reflex/contentCompose.ts:57`) carries `contentId`, `slot`, `order` — and **no
   `decision_id`**. The SDK's `contentClick(contentId, slot, attrs)` sends neither an id nor the order. So
   that clause is a wire + SDK + server change, not a policy change. `slot` is already on the wire and
   already on the outcome record; `order` exists client-side and needs only to be added to the payload. The
   remedy should have said which of the three it was asking for is free and which is not.
4. **"Distinguish served, rendered, viewable impression, click and conversion records" is over-scoped as a
   pilot gate.** It is the right end state. It is a telemetry redesign, and it is not what makes the pilot's
   numbers wrong today.
5. **The impression half is described incompletely.** `listen.rendered()` (`listen.ts:120`) fires on paint,
   but the *other* capture path, `declarative()` (`src/sdk/emit.ts:97-101`), gates the **same**
   `content_impression` on `intersectionRatio >= 0.5` (`src/sdk/host.ts:16-18`). One event name, two
   meanings, in one SDK. And when `IntersectionObserver` is absent, `host.ts:15` calls `cb(true)`
   immediately, degrading the visibility path to the paint path. More importantly: **the impression is not
   used by the learning loop at all** — `rewardOf` (`records.ts:90`) has no `content_impression` row, so
   `outcomeFromAction` returns null, and `contentTelemetry.ts:58` weights it 0 in the affinity engine. There
   is no impression signal to switch exposures over to; it exists on the wire and is thrown away.

None of these overturns the finding.

## 6 · What the audit missed, adjacent to this finding

**(a) `servedCounts` ignores the slot too — and unlike lift, fatigue is live.** `src/learn/fan.ts:50-60`.
Its own JSDoc says "slot → item → times served inside **that slot's** fatigue window". The loop is
`for (const e of ring) if (e.ts >= since …) counts[e.item] = (counts[e.item] ?? 0) + 1` — `e.slot` is never
compared to `s.slot`. Reproduced: ring = hero/A once + rail/A twice gives
`{"hero":{"A":3},"rail":{"A":3}}`. The fatigue penalty is page-wide while the receipt sentence
(`explain.fatigue.served`) implies the slot, and fatigue is a real scoring term, not shadow. The audit's
remedy line "Document whether fatigue is page-wide or slot-specific" treats a code/doc contradiction as a
documentation gap.

**(b) The learning identity has no page.** `statsName(tenant, brand, slot)` and `liftKey(...)`
(`src/learn/fan.ts:15-16`) carry no page, and `validateSlot` forbids duplicate slot names only *within* a
page (`src/content/kinds.ts:145`). So `home/hero` and `pdp/hero` share one statistics object, one baseline
rate, one lift table, one gamma, one reward and one objective. This is the same "the pooling key is coarser
than the placement" defect as position, one level up, and F21 does not mention it.

**(c) `ringEntryOf` drops `brand` and `position`, and online disagrees with batch.**
`src/learn/fan.ts:62-63`. The batch fold deliberately puts brand back —
`BatchRingEntry extends RingEntry { brand: string }` (`hourly.ts:70`), `compactOf` at `:74` — and filters
`ring.filter(e => e.brand === o.brand)` at `:261`. The online path in `DecisionRing.outcome` cannot: the
entry has no brand, so it filters only `arm === 'personalized'`. `compactOf` also keeps `position`, and uses
it solely for the exploration counter (`d.position === 0`, `:202`), never for pooling. So the batch layer
already carries both fields the online layer needs and still does not pool by position.
There is a second half to this. At ingest, `src/routes/realtime.ts:126` calls
`outcomeFromAction({…}, c.get('tenant'))` and lets `brand` default to the tenant, while exposures are fanned
under the `?brand=` the decision request carried (`service.ts:130`). Today `DEFAULT_TENANT = 'coach'` and
nothing sets `?brand=`, so the two agree and this is **latent, not firing**. The first tenant that actually
uses brands — Tapestry, whose contract is Tapestry-wide with Coach as the launch brand — puts the numerator
in one statistics object and the denominator in another.

**(d) A green test locks the defect in — the "passes for the wrong reason" case.**
`src/learn/learn.test.ts:25-28`, titled *"direct match, last credit, one credit per slot"*, asserts
`[['story','d2','click'], ['hero','d1','click']]` — two slots credited for one click. The outcome factory at
`:20` hard-codes `slot: null`, and **no test in the repository passes a slot-bearing outcome to
`attribute()`**. The suite is green precisely because it encodes the behaviour, and the title reads as if it
checked the opposite.

**(e) The receipt overclaims on an unrendered slot.** Probe 5: `n = 3,991`, `level_words = "channel, visit
bucket, stage, region and affinity cell"` for a placement nobody saw. The customer-facing artefact of
"served ≠ seen" is the explain block, not the lift table.

**(f) `impressed.clear()` re-arms the impression dedupe on every applied set.** `src/sdk/listen.ts:57`,
inside `apply()`. An SPA that re-applies a decision set re-sends `content_impression` for unchanged content.
The audit lists "rapid rerenders" as a test to add without naming the line that would fail it.

**(g) One thing the code gets right, worth not breaking.** The five-minute self-monitor
(`src/ops/monitor.ts:68`) requests decisions with `opt_tracking_consent=false`, so `consent.tracking` is
false, `fanDecisions` is skipped, and synthetic monitoring traffic does not pollute the statistics.

## 8 · Remedy assessment and the smallest safe fix

**The audit's remedy is directionally right, mis-scoped at both ends, and incomplete.** Right: slot must
constrain interaction credit; multi-placement and downstream-purchase credit needs its own declared rule;
position must be stratified or the experiment constrained to one placement. Over-scoped: the five-way
served/rendered/viewable/click/conversion taxonomy as a pilot gate. Under-scoped: it names only
`src/learn/policy.ts` and misses (a), (b) and (c) above, and it misses the good news that **one edit to
`attribute()` fixes the online path and the batch/report path together**, because `DecisionRing.outcome:82`
and `hourly.foldShard:266` both call it — so the lift table and the day report cannot drift apart.

**Smallest safe fix that closes the launch-blocking part (S, roughly half a day plus tests):**

1. Add a fifth *declared* axis to `AttributionPolicy` in `src/learn/policy.ts`:
   `placement: 'named' | 'any'`, default `'named'`. Doc 22 §4.1 lists four axes and calls attribution a
   policy; this keeps it one.
2. In `attribute()`'s filter, when `policy.placement === 'named'`, the reward is an interaction reward
   (`click`, `dwell`, `video_complete`) **and** `outcome.slot` is a usable slot, require
   `e.slot === outcome.slot`. Fall back to today's behaviour otherwise, so product-named rewards
   (`purchase`, `add_to_bag`, `wishlist`) keep the CW32 featured-product rule and clients that send no slot
   keep learning.
3. **Treat `'unknown'` as absent.** `src/sdk/emit.ts:93` defaults a missing `data-op-slot` to the literal
   string `'unknown'`. Without this guard the fix silently stops crediting every declaratively-tagged site
   that omits the attribute. This is the one way a naive version of this change makes things worse.
4. Add the missing test — a slot-bearing outcome through `attribute()` — and rewrite the misleading title at
   `src/learn/learn.test.ts:25`.
5. One line in `src/learn/fan.ts:57`: `if (e.slot === s.slot && e.ts >= since && e.ts <= now)`. Fatigue is
   live, its JSDoc already promises per-slot, and this is the cheapest true statement in the finding.

That closes "direct does not mean the clicked placement" and the fatigue contradiction without touching the
wire, the Durable Objects, the record schema or the ladder.

**What must still follow before any slot runs at `gamma > 0`, before any autonomy proposal is accepted, and
before lift is shown to the customer as evidence of incremental value** — this is the M:

- Position in the statistics identity for multi-item slots, per doc 22 §5.5 and the per-slot dial doc 22
  already assigns to Data Science (line 668). `ringEntryOf` must stop dropping `position`; `compactOf`
  already keeps it.
- Exposures gated on a rendered — better, viewable — impression, which requires putting the decision id (or
  slot + order) on the client decision and on the impression event, and a dedupe in `LearnStats`.
- Page in `statsName`/`liftKey`, or an enforced global uniqueness of slot names across pages.
- `brand` on the online `RingEntry`, and the outcome's brand resolved from the decision rather than defaulted
  to the tenant at `src/routes/realtime.ts:126`.

**Until that lands, the honest position for the pilot** is the brief's own escape clause: leave `gamma` at 0,
and report the lift table as a diagnostic that is not corrected for position or placement — not as evidence
of incremental business lift. The audit's opening verdict already says this; F21 is one of the reasons it is
right.
