## 2 · Corrections (what §4 gets wrong, precisely)

1. **Row 1 — the 48–72-hour rebuttal has no target.** No delivery document claims 48–72-hour convergence. Doc 26 §A.3.5 quotes it as the customer's requirement and answers that the 21-day `tauLearnMs` covers it "at a slower pace, and it is a dial". Keep the point; attribute it to the requirement, not to a claim by the team.
2. **Row 1 — "not confidence guarantees" restates the design rather than correcting it.** Doc 22 §5.1 defines n₀ as prior strength and n_min as an evidence gate, and §13 already books τ_learn, ladder order and n_min as Data Science-owned tunables.
3. **Row 10 — "not an SSO claim" rebuts a claim nobody made.** Brief §5.10 and doc 30 §4 both state SSO and invitations do not exist and that the customer chooses.
4. **Row 2 — "missing visit/channel input" misnames the defect.** The dimensions are built and persisted (`src/services/visit.ts`, `SessionManager.ts:214`); they are simply not carried into `ShopperRead`/`cellFor`. Saying "missing input" invites a rebuild estimate for what is a plumbing change — and it lets doc 22 §18.8's stale "Resolved 2026-09-02 … Phase 1 can pool on them" stand unchallenged, which is the statement that is actually false.
5. **Row 3 — "recompute versioned generations" over-claims absence.** Reporting-policy recomputation over the ledger already exists for a day (`report.ts` `grids[slot][policy.name]`). The gap is the online counters and cross-day generations; say that.
6. **Row 6 — the row never states the ring's real bound.** The brief's "the online ring holds seven days" is only true below 200 entries (`RING_MAX = 200`, `DecisionRing.ts`). §3 of the audit says it; §4's row 3/row 6 pair does not, and the brief's sentence is the one a reader will carry away.

## 3 · Additions (what §4 should have said and did not)

1. **Host choice silently disables learning, not just consent/erase (rows 5, 7).** On `REFLEX_HOST = 'do'`, `readShopper` returns `sessionId: null` and `isNewSession: null`. `session_id` then depends entirely on the client (`sessionId: r.sessionId || shopper.sessionId`), and `policy.ts:74-79` refuses to credit when either side is null. Exposures keep accruing, credits stop, and every item's lift decays below 1. That belongs in row 7's "invariants" list.
2. **Cell cardinality is integrator-controlled (rows 1, 2).** `cellFor` accepts any caller string as the channel — `.trim().toLowerCase().slice(0, 32) || 'unknown'` — and the SDK types it as free text (`channel?: string`, `src/sdk/listen.ts:12`, `:75`; its own test asserts `channel=paid%20social`, which is not the platform's `paid_social`). F08's 2 MB ceiling can therefore be reached through a misconfigured integration, not only through affinity growth.
3. **The working prior path still cannot move a ranking in the cold case (row 1).** Even at the correctly indexed root key, `buildSnapshot` sets `lift = 1` whenever `p0slot <= 0`, so an imported prior on a slot with no live exposures yields `p̂ = 0.2` and `lift = 1.0` (reproduced). Fixing the key split alone does not deliver cold start.
4. **Autonomy runs off the wrong tenant list (rows 5, 12).** The daily cycle is driven by `TREND_ROLLUP_TENANTS ?? 'coach'` — an environment variable named for a different job — while every other scheduled job uses the provisioned registry.
5. **The console's pin is not editable at all (row 11).** Row 11 asks for "the business-user weight/pin workflow"; the shipped view displays the pin as text. And its default stage rule cannot be saved against the production validator (reproduced), so the one weight-shaped control the merchandiser can reach is broken on first use.
6. **The default arm mixes three populations (row 4).** Randomized controls, consent-refusers (`armUnder`) and pinned merchandising all present as `arm: 'default'`, so even a corrected estimator reads a blended control.
7. **The credit's money value is computed in two places (rows 3, 6).** `attribute()` returns `weight: 1`; `DecisionRing.outcome()` and `report.ts:118` each apply `creditWeight` independently. Live/batch divergence has a second source beyond duplicate delivery.
