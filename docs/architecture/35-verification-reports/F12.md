## 3 · What the audit got wrong

1. **A wrong line.** `src/sdk/core.ts:225` is `case 'odp_receipt'`. The `content_decisions` case is 226. One line.
2. **The title overstates.** "Never repaints" reads as an SDK incapacity. `listen.hydrate()` is public, documented, idempotent, and repaints correctly on a second call (proved above). The accurate title is *the published integration has no repaint trigger, and the frame the kit says will trigger it is never sent.*
3. **The consequence is true but reads broader than it is.** "The server's affinity can change while the customer's content stays unchanged" is correct **within a page view**. A navigation or reload re-hydrates and picks up the new decision; the server's re-decision itself is correct and is proved by `scripts/acceptance-run.mjs:159-165` ("second snapshot sees the stage"; "the piece made for a considering shopper now wins"). The kit's own §8 test 4 (`01-integration-guide.md:181`) says *"View a product, **reload the page**: the hero follows the interest"* — the shipped acceptance test is already consistent with a page-load-scoped model. The engine is not broken; the client contract and the kit are.
4. **Size M is over-scoped for the gate it blocks.** See §6.

Nothing else in the finding is wrong. Every other citation is exact.

## 4 · What the audit missed

**a. The strongest citation was not used.** `docs/kit/01-integration-guide.md:156` is a row in the §6 "live channel" table:

> \| `content_decisions` \| when the platform re-decides a page \| applied through `listen` like the first snapshot; your slot subscribers fire again \|

That is the kit telling the customer's engineers, in the document they integrate from, that a frame arrives which no code sends. The same promise is repeated at `docs/kit/02-api-reference.md:65`, `docs/kit/03-payload-schemas.md:185`, `src/sdk/README.md:124` and `ONBOARDING.md:109`. This is the citation that makes the finding unarguable, and the audit reasoned to it from the sender side instead.

**b. A test that passes for the wrong reason — and it is the team's own headline evidence.** `scripts/rehearse-storefront.sh:42` drives `/storefront?sdk=1` in a headless browser, presses `#dir-next` four times, and `:84` asserts *"the hero follows the line she circles."* It passes. It passes because of `public/storefront.js:354`'s private 400 ms rehydrate, not because the SDK repaints. The rehearsal that was built to prove the SDK transport is equivalent to the demo's own actually proves the demo's private orchestration works. Two unit tests do the same at a smaller scale (`listen.test.ts:64`, `core.test.ts:74`).

**c. The obvious fix is actively harmful as the platform is wired today — the audit's remedy does not say so.** An exposure is written **at decision time, once per `serveContentDecisions` call**: `src/content/service.ts:242` → `src/learn/fan.ts:79` posts `/exposures` to `LEARN_STATS`, and `src/durable-objects/LearnStats.ts:35-43` records every one with no dedupe on decision id, item or session. `n` is the denominator of the lift estimate in `src/learn/stats.ts:158`, `p_hat = (s + n0·target) / (n + n0)`, and `nMin` (30, per brief §5.1) is the gate the pooling ladder descends on. Therefore any refresh-based repaint multiplies the denominator without touching the numerator: it depresses the served item's estimate, drags the slot rate down with it, and reaches 30 "exposures" without 30 human impressions. The demo already does this — up to four snapshot calls for one painted hero across three spaced product views. Meanwhile the SDK's own `content_impression` (`src/sdk/listen.ts:120`) never reaches the statistics at all. **A remedy that adds a refresh loop and does not touch the exposure accounting will quietly corrupt the lift table the pilot is meant to demonstrate.**

**d. Three present defects in the same 140 lines, all of which the fix will hit immediately.** Probes in `/tmp/audit-verify/F12/adjacent-defects.mjs`, run against the published bundle:

- **Stale-response overwrite.** `src/sdk/listen.ts:93` applies the snapshot unconditionally after the await. Two overlapping hydrates land in completion order, not issue order. Observed: `paints: NEW -> OLD`, `listen.current().ts === 1000` — the *older* snapshot won. The audit lists "discard stale responses" as a remedy; it is a defect present now, and the moment any refresh is added it becomes routine, not exotic.
- **No ordering or dedupe guard on `content_decisions`.** `core.ts:226` returns before `applyIncoming`, so the `lastAppliedTs` dedupe at `core.ts:112-116` never applies to decision frames. Observed: a frame with `ts: 1` overwrote a snapshot with `ts: 5000`, and a duplicate frame repainted a second time. If the push is built as the remedy asks, it ships with this hole.
- **Impression dedupe reset on every repaint.** `src/sdk/listen.ts:57` clears `impressed` inside `apply()`. Observed: three refreshes of an *unchanged* hero produced three `content_impression` POSTs. The demo hits this today through `_renderEngineSlots` (`public/storefront.js:311-329`), which calls `listen.rendered(...)` on every set.

**e. Requirement provenance — the push was a named requirement that fell out of the tracking.** `docs/architecture/19-tapestry-delivery-ledger.md:59` names R3 as *"Ranker + `content_decisions` push + snapshot"*, status **GAP for content**. Its "Remains" column lists the ranker, the page assembler and the first-paint variant — the push is not in it. `docs/architecture/21-tapestry-gap-closure-plan.md:91` then marks CW8 ✅ citing "`content_decisions` frames" as built. Both statements are individually defensible and together they lost the server half of a named requirement.

**f. The repainting integration is not the default.** `public/storefront.js:248-252`: the SDK transport is behind `?sdk=1` or a meta tag. The demo that ships runs its own transport. So the only code in the repository that repaints from an event is a non-default path in a demo the customer never sees.

## 5 · Severity and size

**Severity: blocks the pilot.** I agree with the audit. On the brief's scale this is "the promised customer integration": the kit at `docs/kit/01-integration-guide.md` is the artefact Tapestry's front-end engineers integrate from, and it documents a live repaint that does not exist. It is not a live-data-safety or production-reliability issue, so it is not *blocks launch* — with one caveat worth stating to the delivery lead: the brief's *blocks launch* band also covers "representations that the full contracted capability is delivered," and a kit table asserting a frame the platform never sends is exactly such a representation. The correction is due **before the kit is sent**, which the commit history (`381eeb2`) places at Kickoff + 45.

**Size: S for the part that blocks the pilot; M only if the genuine push is in scope.** The audit's M is right for the remedy *as the audit scopes it* (build the push, coalesce, staleness-guard, full test matrix) but wrong as a price on the pilot gate, because the server already re-decides correctly and the SDK already repaints on a second `hydrate()`. The exposure-accounting collision (§4c) is the one thing that keeps the honest fix from being pure documentation, and it is a small, contained change.

## 6 · Assessment of the remedy, and the smallest safe fix

The audit's remedy is **directionally right, over-scoped on delivery, and under-scoped on one dimension.**

- *"Make page-scoped refresh/subscription a supported SDK contract **or** implement genuine content-decision push"* — correct, and the disjunction is the right one. The first branch is a day's work; the second is the right end state.
- *"Coalesce bursts, wait for authoritative state, and discard stale responses"* — correct, and §4d shows two of the three are defects present today rather than requirements for new work.
- *"Test the exact published snippet through event → decision → rendered IDs, including reconnects, concurrent events, and account transitions"* — correct and necessary; §4b shows why the existing rehearsal does not count.
- **Missing:** the remedy does not mention that an exposure is written per snapshot call. Implemented naively — a rehydrate on each event — it will inflate the learning denominator and degrade the lift evidence the pilot exists to show.

**Smallest safe fix that closes the launch-blocking part (S, ~1–2 engineer-days):**

1. **Correct the kit before it is sent.** Strike or mark "not implemented" the `content_decisions` frame at `docs/kit/01-integration-guide.md:156`, `docs/kit/02-api-reference.md:65`, `docs/kit/03-payload-schemas.md:185`, `src/sdk/README.md:124`, `ONBOARDING.md:109`. Say plainly, in §3, that decisions are page-load- and refresh-scoped today.
2. **Give the integrator the trigger, in the ten lines.** Add a documented `client.listen.refresh()` — a coalescing wrapper over `hydrate` (300–500 ms debounce) that guards `apply()` with `set.ts` so a late or older response is discarded (`src/sdk/listen.ts:55`, `:93`), and does not reset `impressed` when the decision set is unchanged (`:57`). That closes three of §4d's defects in the same edit. Show it called after a meaningful interaction and on route change.
3. **Stop the refresh from writing an exposure.** Either a non-counting snapshot mode used by `refresh()`, or move the exposure write to `content_impression` (which the SDK already sends, `listen.ts:120`, and which the statistics currently ignore). Without this, step 2 corrupts the lift table.

Steps 1 and 2 make the published contract true. Step 3 keeps it from costing the measurement. The genuine push, with reconnect/concurrency/account-transition tests, remains the right end state and is the M the audit priced — it is not the pilot gate.
