The lift grid

Every item the slot has served, in every cell it was seen in. n is decayed exposures, s decayed successes on the slot’s reward, p̂ the smoothed rate (successes plus the prior, over exposures plus the prior’s strength n₀), p₀ the slot’s own rate in that cell, the baseline, and lift is p̂ over p₀, clamped. Evidence is n over n plus n₀: how much of the estimate is live observation rather than the prior.

What is exploring

Items below the slot’s observation floor are the ones exploration serves on purpose. The realized share comes from the day report and is checked against the configured one.

Policy comparison

Both ledgers are immutable, so attribution is a read: the same day under the learning policy and any reporting policy, side by side, without changing what is served. The same pass reports the holdout arms against each other. Built from the R2 ledger, never from analytics.
