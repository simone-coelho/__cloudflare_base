Behavioral Targeting and Intelligence: see it work

For marketing: the engine at work on one sample store, in seven steps to walk through before writing or speaking about it.

What we built

We built an engine that follows each visitor's interest in real time and decides the page at that point in time.

  • Most visitors to a brand's site arrive as strangers, and most buying happens before any profile exists. Tools built for known customers are blind in that window.

  • The engine watches what a visitor does on this visit and arranges the page around it, click by click, from the first page on.

  • The brand's team publishes the rules, keeps the last word, and can read the reason behind every decision.

How it differs from recommendations and from personalization, and why

  • A recommendations engine

    Ranks the past: what people like this visitor bought before.

    History → Ranks products → Product row

    The engine

    Decides the page from what this visitor is doing now. The brand's carousel is one of the modules it orders.

    Observe this visit → Interpret interest → Act on the page

    Recommendations rank the past; the engine decides the page at the point in time, and the two work side by side.

  • Rule-based personalization

    Sorts visitors into segments written in advance. The rules go stale, multiply and fight.

    The engine

    Needs no segment and no expiration rule. Interest fills with each action and drains on its own, like a bucket.

  • AI that decides

    Chooses in the moment and cannot show its work.

    The engine

    Every decision is arithmetic a person can recompute, on a receipt.

    The engine calls no AI model to decide. Opal is where people ask and generative AI creates, and that is where credits apply.

The first page feels local

Morrow & Vale is a sample store invented for this tour, and what visitors in each region lean toward is invented too.

Before any click, the engine knows only the visitor's region. The sections visitors there lean toward come first, and the hero follows the strongest lean.

The first click changes the page

A click on a product pours into the bucket for what it touches, such as knitwear. An add to the bag counts three times a look, so that bucket passes its entry line at once.

Two sections trade places on the next page, and the receipt records the click.

We aim to make each decision within about 200 milliseconds at the edge; on average we see 50 to 250 milliseconds, and tens of milliseconds once the engine has seen the visitor.

A second interest fills

A look at something different fills a second bucket partway. One look is a clue, not a conclusion.

The page leans on both: the first interest still leads, and the second one's section climbs past the brand's recommendations carousel.

The first bucket drains, and the visitor is released

Every bucket drains on its own, and nobody writes an expiration rule. Below its release line, the visitor is released from that interest, its section drops back, and the receipt records the release.

This sample store keeps interest for five minutes so the tour can show it fade; each brand sets how long its buckets hold, and the engine's default is 14 days.

A pin holds its place

A merchandiser sold the partner spotlight the first place on the home page, so the brand pins it there. The brand's rules come first, and the engine ranks the rest around them.

The pinned section holds its place while the others reorder, and the receipt says ranking never ran for it.

Every decision comes with a receipt

The receipt shows what was served, in which place, and the arithmetic behind it, so a person can recompute the page by hand.

Each score blends the visitor's own interest with the region's lean. The less the engine has seen of the visitor, the more the region counts.

Learning waits for the brand's dial

It records what works from the first day and changes nothing until the brand turns the dial.

A holdout keeps a share of visitors on the site's own page, so the brand measures the lift on its own numbers. The whole story is on What it is, and every constant has an instrument on How it works, interactive.
